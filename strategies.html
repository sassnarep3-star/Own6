Candidate engineering principles

Separate opportunity from conversion

For player props, model playing time and opportunities before outcomes. A shot-quality measure such as NBA xFG% can help describe attempts, but does not guarantee future makes. Definitions are sourced; predictive lift is unverified.

Price distributions, not vibes

Estimate a full predictive distribution, include parameter uncertainty, and compare a probability to a contemporaneous price after accounting for vig and execution. No quote or edge is currently published.
