- Every injury row links to `https://www.nfl.com/injuries/` (347 links, checked by `scripts/check_nfl_s11.py`).

- Reconciliation values are generated by `scripts/build_nfl_s11.py` from the transcription and the repo CSVs. Their counts are pinned (347 / 14 / 28 / 14 players / 28 fields / 21 roster gaps / 13 venues / 14 flags) and drift fails the check.
- `python3 -m unittest tests.test_nfl_s11` runs 14 tests; `python3 scripts/check_nfl_s11.py` returns OK.
- Blank cells are blank, never inferred. A blank game status means the page printed none (176 rows, F-13).
