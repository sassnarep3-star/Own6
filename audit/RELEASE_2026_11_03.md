## Gates and boundaries

- For a market candidate: rights → source provenance → pre-event chronology → effective sample size → market-relative proper scores → post-vig return with clustered uncertainty → closing-line comparison → multiplicity control → dependent-leg joint modeling → immutable experiment log. All ten must pass; no wins are inferred from positive point estimates alone.
- First-party executable odds, permitted NBA features, settlement evidence and append-only pre-event timestamps are absent. Without them, the system cannot honestly publish an edge, ROI, empirical correlation or reward. A hyperlink to an official glossary is not evidence of an executable price.
- The repaired Flagged for Review page publishes the inherited legal/odds limitations and distinguishes third-party historical quotes from first-party evidence. The root dashboard directs users there.
- Outstanding risk: advisory source gaps in earlier separately owned pages and changing terms/rights. They are review items, not silent verification claims. No betting advice or real reward is issued.
