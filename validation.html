How to estimate correlation

For outcomes X and Y, estimate Pearson correlation from paired, synchronized observations (or standardized out-of-sample residuals): Cov(X,Y)/(SD(X)·SD(Y)). Report sample size, confidence interval, time window, player/role context and shrinkage method. For binary over/under outcomes, also report the joint event probability and conditional rates; raw correlation alone does not determine parlay value. This is a proposed statistical method, not an observed NBA value.

Current correlation: Blank — unverified / no paired dataset

Leaderboard scoring

Keep an immutable timestamped prediction ledger. Report net ROI with uncertainty, proper probabilistic scores, calibration, benchmark closing-line movement, drawdown and eligible sample size. Rank using a preregistered risk-adjusted metric with confidence-aware eligibility; raw win percentage is insufficient. Exact reward metric and thresholds remain unspecified.

Validation design is proposed; no backtest or measured correlation is claimed.
