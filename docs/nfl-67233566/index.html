Read this first. This pack was captured in the middle of a live week. Three Week 10 games were still being played and two had not started, so the backtest corpus is 145 finished games — 136 from Weeks 1-9, reconciled line by line against the official standings and card badges read here, plus the nine Week 10 finals. And the Week 11 injury report did not exist yet: the report page’s own selector still read WEEK 1..WEEK 10, and the Week 11 game centre prints “Injury Report is not available at this time” for both clubs. So nothing in this pack claims an availability-based edge on Week 11 — that is a fact about the source, not a gap in the model.

Where the decisions actually are

Most open gates are not modelling problems. 10 of the 79 traced requirements turn on a price at all, and they share one gate: G-08, one of the 3 open or failing rows on the gate board. Of the 17 requirements blocked for want of a source, exactly 1 is the missing price itself - the other sixteen are capture and coverage problems, not procurement. Until a price exists, the ROI, CLV and promotion questions are unanswerable at any effort level, and the honest move is to keep the column blank. The list, not this sentence, is requirements_trace.csv; both counts are recomputed from the published tables on every build.
The rest is a capture problem. One dated read of the injury page cannot support a surprise signal, a replay or an audit. This pack publishes the first row of that series and the diff machinery that will use it.
