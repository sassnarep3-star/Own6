48-vs-47 Active Roster & 8-OL Rule

Under NFL Football Operations contract language, dressing fewer than 8 offensive linemen drops a club's gameday active limit from 48 to 47 players — compounding multi-OL injury clusters (Patriots & Cardinals in Week 10).

Emergency 3rd QB & PS Elevation Caps

Emergency 3rd QBs must sit on the 53-man roster (Practice Squad QBs are ineligible per NFLPA veto) and enter only if QB1 & QB2 exit via injury/disqualification. Standard PS elevations cap at 2/week and 3 games/season/player.
