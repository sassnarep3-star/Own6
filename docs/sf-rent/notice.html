Notice & fair practice

A privacy-conscious review of two reported rent-controlled units in the Outer Sunset. Official links support legal rules, not independently unverified private tenancy facts. Confirm current law and records before acting.

TopicFinding & statusManual review
Written noticeGive at least 30 days for an increase of 10% or less, or 90 days if the increase alone or combined with increases in the preceding year exceeds 10%. Add five days if mailed; confirm proper service and lease requirements.Official source ↗
ContentsIdentify the dollar and percentage increase and effective date; identify the annual and any banked components in writing. Verify the correct recipient, service and legal language with counsel.Official source ↗
ReasonsFor an ordinary annual allowance, an honest explanation is a routine annual adjustment under the Rent Ordinance; do not claim documented expense increases or improvements without evidence. Special cost-based claims require their own procedure.Official source ↗
Fair housingDo not base differences on protected characteristics, including disability, race, familial status or source of income; do not retaliate for protected activity. Consistency helps but cannot eliminate liability.Official source ↗
Owner typesOfficial evidence of typical actual increases by large corporate owners versus individual SF owners is unverified. Ownership size does not by itself establish an applicable increase or normal rate. No comparative percentage is asserted.Official source ↗
