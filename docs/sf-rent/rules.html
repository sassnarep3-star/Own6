Law and calculations

A privacy-conscious review of two reported rent-controlled units in the Outer Sunset. Official links support legal rules, not independently unverified private tenancy facts. Confirm current law and records before acting.

TopicFinding & statusManual review
Local annual ruleSF permits an allowable annual increase generally once every 12 months. The applicable rate depends on effective date; calculate on lawful base rent, excluding temporary passthroughs, without rounding up to whole dollars.Official source ↗
Current and prior rates1.6%: March 1, 2026–February 28, 2027; 1.4%: March 1, 2025–February 28, 2026. The rate after February 28, 2027 is unverified here.Official source ↗
BankingGenerally a skipped increase banks only after 24 full months without an increase; partially unused increases have separate rules. Add eligible percentages, do not compound or prorate. Off-anniversary timing may forfeit an increment.Official source ↗
CaliforniaSF says AB 1482’s 8.8% rate for August 1, 2026–July 31, 2027 does not apply to units under SF rent increase limitations. It does not displace local protections. Do not substitute that figure for the local allowance.Official source ↗
FederalFair Housing Act protections prohibit discriminatory rental terms and retaliation for protected fair-housing activity; covered subsidy programs may impose additional restrictions. Federal fair-housing rules are not a general authorization to exceed local limits.Official source ↗
