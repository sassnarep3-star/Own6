Reward policy

Paper units only; no money. The earlier schedule (1st +20, 2nd +10, 3rd +5 paper units) is retained as a design, but this hub pays nothing until a strategy is eligible: ≥100 graded events, calibration CI strictly above zero versus the baseline, and a market-relative (CLV) result once closing lines exist. Reward design carries legal exposure (flags F01/F02): public reward contests tied to sports results were flagged for counsel. Source: research/strategies.md · data/strategies.json.
