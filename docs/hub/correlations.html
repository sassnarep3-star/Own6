Correlations

Measured dependence between legs, with sample sizes and uncertainty.

Correlation is what makes parlays and prop combos non-independent: pricing a combination as the product of marginal probabilities is wrong when r ≠ 0. Values are Pearson r from official results (and ESPN game logs for the one player pair). Intervals are Fisher-z 95% intervals, computed here from r and n. Only rows with n ≥ 30 are treated as usable; the teammate pair (n = 6) is not. Leg-level model-error correlations cannot be estimated yet (no settled history of bets).
