Executive summary

What was built, what was found, what is and is not verified.

Bottom line: no Own6 strategy has shown a statistically supported edge. The first dated forward test (15 games) found the market at least as accurate as Own6 on margin, total and win probability, with all intervals including zero. No profitability claim is made, no rewards are paid, and no wager is placed.
