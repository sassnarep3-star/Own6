Why a record alone proves nothing

A leaderboard rewards the strategy that looks best. Luck guarantees that one candidate looks best even when no candidate has an edge: with 200 tested candidates at alpha 0.05, about ten pass by chance alone. This section computes, in closed form, the sample sizes and corrections that separate a real edge from that noise, and then applies the rule to illustrative records.

A 55%/100-bet record looks excellent and is indistinguishable from luck: the p-value is 0.336 and the Wilson lower bound sits below the 52.38% break-even rate. Even 540 wins in 1,000 bets fails. Only when the sample grows to roughly 560 of 1,000 does the interval clear break-even.
