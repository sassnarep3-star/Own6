MODEL

Own-line origination

Fair values originated from verified scoring, with the assumptions exposed as a grid instead of hidden in a constant.

Lines originated13
Market columns populated0 (by design)
Sign stable across the grid11 of 13
Total invariance to home pointsyes, by construction

Method: each club's points scored and allowed per game come from the standings register; a game's expected total is the average of the two clubs' scoring and each other's allowing, the spread is the difference, and the win, push and key-number probabilities come from the exact margin distribution of two Poisson laws - so a tie is a priced outcome rather than a rounding error.

The arithmetic, in full

base_home = 0.5 * (home.points_per_game + away.points_allowed_per_game)
base_away = 0.5 * (away.points_per_game + home.points_allowed_per_game)
expected_total = base_home + base_away
expected_margin = base_home - base_away + home_field_points   (0 on this slate, and 0 at a neutral site)
points_home ~ Poisson(base_home + hfp/2), points_away ~ Poisson(base_away - hfp/2)
P(margin = m) = sum_k P_home(k + m) * P_away(k)        (exact, truncated at 80 points and renormalised)
P(home win) = sum over m > 0, plus half the mass at m = 0

Two assumptions are load-bearing and labelled: the Poisson law for points, and zero home-field points. The first is a distributional choice, the second is a refusal to import a number this register cannot measure. Both are varied in the grid below; the market columns stay blank because there is no licensed quote to fill them (FL-01).
