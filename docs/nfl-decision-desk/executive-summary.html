What this pack adds over the three published NFL packs

  • An actual re-verification: 544 field-by-field comparisons of the prior registers against today's official read, with a disposition for each.
  • Originated lines that are computed rather than deferred: fair spread, total, win and push probability for all 13 unsettled games, from verified scoring only.
  • Luck instrumented with numbers: Pythagorean fit, residual variance against a binomial floor, and a cross-season persistence test.
  • Correlations measured where they can be, and bounded where they cannot - no invented coefficients.
  • The full Week 10 injury report for 28 clubs, transcribed and scored into 77 club-unit deficiency records.
  • A leaderboard that separates readiness (rankable today) from performance (empty until the evidence gates pass), with the reward ladder published before anyone competes.

The two decisions that unblock the most

  1. Start the weekly snapshot job (SUG-03). Four dated, hashed captures per slate unlock DG-04, and after roughly eight slates DG-06 and DG-15 as well. It needs no licence and no money.
  2. Answer the rights question in writing (SUG-04, Q-01). A licensed quote feed is the only way to DG-03, DG-07, DG-08 and DG-13 - and a documented refusal is a legitimate outcome, because it converts an invisible blocker into a scope decision.

Both are cheap; neither is urgent until a third of the site's value depends on them, which is exactly where the project now sits.

Boundaries this pack will not cross

  • No line, vig, ROI, CLV or skill score is published without an authorized source - those cells are blank and listed on the flagged page.
  • Model assumptions (Poisson law, zero home points, ordinal depth weights) are labelled in-cell and are excluded from every gate.
  • Nothing here is a pick. The desk prices an internal estimate and states the evidence still missing before that estimate deserves trust.

Every table row links to the official page actually read for it. A blank cell means no confirmed value exists, not zero. Values labelled model assumption are the model's own dials and are never presented as facts.
