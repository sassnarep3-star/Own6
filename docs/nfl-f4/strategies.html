Structures, and what each one would need

Single (S1–S4)
One market, one price. Needs a verified open, a verified close and a verified result per bet.
Parlay, multi-game (S6)
Two independent legs. The advertised payout implies an independence assumption that this pack refuses to assume — the correlation table is the input, and it currently says the side/total dependence is not distinguishable from zero at n=137 (r = −0.16, p = 0.06).
Same-game parlay (S5)
Legs that are structurally correlated. Without a measured same-game correlation, an SGP price is a guess. Gate G4 blocks it.
Player-prop combo (S7)
Requires official player-level stat lines per game, which this pack does not capture. Blocked at G5 rather than filled with a plausible number.

Rewards

The reward for a top-performing strategy is rank and promotion eligibility — nothing else. No staking plan is published, because a staking plan is the fastest way to convert a signal claim into a variance test. The moment a strategy clears the gates, its reward becomes a live slot with the demotion gate attached.
