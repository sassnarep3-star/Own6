Capture protocol

  • The provider's own open and close (or current) objects are captured, so open and close are two fields of one atomic read rather than two visits that may straddle a move.
  • Every snapshot stores the price as well as the line. A line that moved 3 points at even money is a different event from a line that moved 3 points through a key number with the vig attached.
  • Capture time is recorded per snapshot and per read date. The store is append-only by convention: a re-read adds snapshots, it does not overwrite.
  • Provider identity is stored on every row. The feed is the operator's own published price relayed by a public endpoint — an operator quote, not an NFL publication, and the distinction is written into sources.csv.
Sources are linked on every row. A blank cell is a cell that no official source confirmed; it is never a zero and never an estimate. This pack is informational and non-commercial. It is not betting advice, and it makes no profitability claim.
