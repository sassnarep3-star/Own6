Decision questions

What we know, what we do not know, and what would settle it — question by question.

Why this page exists. A dashboard that only shows what it knows is a confidence machine. This page lists the questions that have to be answered before any decision on this site is worth making, separates the ones the data already answers from the ones it cannot, and says for each open question exactly what would close it and where that would come from.
16 questions: 12 answered from verified data, 4 open.

Q01Can a strategy's edge be measured at all yet?No. One settled priced game and 137 scored games without prices. Every strategy fails gate G1.answeredAccumulate settled closes; the price store already records them.
Q02Is the results feed trustworthy enough to grade against?Yes for scores: 32 of 32 teams match official standings on W, L, T, PF and PA.answeredNothing further for scores; a play-by-play feed would be needed for overtime and drive data.
Q03Do we have real opening and closing numbers?Yes for 14 games: 42 markets, 168 snapshots, each with the price as well as the line.answeredA second provider's close for the same games would allow a consensus close.
Q04Is the side/total correlation strong enough to price a same-game parlay?Not established: r = -0.161 at n = 137, p = 0.061, interval spanning zero.answeredRoughly 400+ games, or a same-game-specific sample.
Q05Should offence and defence be modelled together?No: points scored and allowed in the same team-game correlate at 0.075 (n = 274).answeredNothing; this is a stable negative result.
Q06Does rest differential matter?Unknown — it is not computable from the sources captured.openCapture a per-team rest column from an official schedule with game dates and bye weeks reconciled.
Q07How much does losing a starter to the third string cost?Unknown. We can name the chain and the string, but not a price.openCollect depth snapshots weekly across a season, then regress margin on deficiency score.
Q08Are the depth charts authoritative?No. Both clubs label them unofficial in their own words.answeredAn official league depth feed, if one is published, would replace them.
Q09Is the closing line available before kickoff for every game?No. Thirteen games had not kicked off at capture, so no close existed to record.answeredRe-read the odds endpoint after each kickoff window; the store is append-only.
Q10Would a lucky staking run mask a bad signal on this board?The guard fires by construction and is unit-tested; on the live board no masking case exists yet, which is a small-sample artefact.answeredNothing; the test is the evidence until real samples exist.
Q11Which operator's price should be the reference?DraftKings, because it is the only operator with an open/close pair for all 14 games.answeredA multi-book consensus, which needs at least two operators captured the same minute.
Q12Is the model good enough to quote against a close?No. It is a naive rating with no opponent adjustment; its disagreements are evidence about the rating, not the line.openOpponent-adjusted ratings with a declared fitting window, then the same gate set.
Q13What happens when a live strategy decays?It is demoted by rules D1-D6 with thresholds fixed in advance.answeredNothing; monitor the state file.
Q14Can prop combos be priced?No. No official player-level stat lines were captured.openAn official stats feed with per-game player lines, then a prop-level backtest.
Q15Does the repo have NFL prices anywhere else?No. Both prior NFL packs leave market cells blank and say so.answeredNothing.
Q16How many clubs have usable depth data?Two of 32, both unofficial.answeredRead the remaining clubs' published charts, or find an official feed.
