Origination order

Side, then total, then moneyline — enforced in the data, not just described.

The order, and why it is a gate

  1. Side first. Deepest price history, least model risk. If the side cannot beat the close, nothing downstream earns the right to a bigger claim.
  2. Total second. Derived from a separate quantity (scoring environment), so it can be checked against the market's own two-way price rather than against the side.
  3. Moneyline last. A nonlinear function of the first two.
