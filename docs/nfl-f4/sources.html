Sources

Every source used, what it supports, and what it explicitly does not support.

Two classes of source. Official means published by the league (nfl.com, operations.nfl.com) or by a club (patriots.com, detroitlions.com). Operator means a licensed sportsbook's own published price — reliable as a price, but not a league publication, and labelled accordingly. Boundary notes say what each source does not support, which is the column that stops a source being over-read.
