Why a staking run can be excluded even when it wins

Fifteen winners at even money happen by chance about once in 32,000 attempts — but you only ever see the attempt that happened. The board therefore computes the probability of a run at least this good under a fair coin, and anything above p = 0.05 is labelled indistinguishable from luck and cannot be promoted, whatever the units column says. Staking is reported because you asked for it; it is never allowed to outrank signal.
