Own lines: fair vs market

The model's fair line, the market's line, and the difference — three columns, always separate.

Read the difference column, not the fair line. A fair line on its own invites belief. The difference column is the only column that can be wrong in a way you can check: it says how far our number is from the market's, and the market's number is the one with money behind it.

Why the moneyline is listed last

Win probability is a function of the fair spread and the total. Publishing it first would mean publishing an assumption as if it were an input. The file is sorted by origination_order by the builder, so a manual edit cannot put the moneyline above the side.

Model status

Every row is model_output. Nothing on this page is verified, and nothing on it claims to be: a model number can be sourced in its inputs but not in its conclusion. The declared parameters behind it — home-field advantage, shrinkage, the margin dispersion — are listed on Validation gates and in flagged.csv.
