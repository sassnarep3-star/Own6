Demotion gate

The defined path back from live to paper, with thresholds fixed before the fact.

A promotion gate without a demotion gate is a ratchet. The thresholds below are fixed in advance so that the exit condition cannot be renegotiated after a strategy starts losing.

Lifecycle

proposed → paper → live → (demotion) → paper → (repeat) → retired

  • D1 Signal decay. Trailing CLV per bet at or below zero over at least 40 settled bets. Winning bets with negative CLV are still decay.
  • D2 Drawdown. More than 8 units below the peak. A staking plan that needs a recovery run to be viable was never viable.
  • D3 Negative run. Three consecutive negative-signal weeks, even if the cumulative number is still positive.
  • D4 Luck divergence. Positive ROI with a non-positive signal — the mirror of the leaderboard mask test. This is the rule that catches the lucky run while it is still winning.
  • D5 Unverifiable. Any live bet without a verified open, close and result. Missing data counts against the strategy, never for it.
  • D6 Model drift. Declared parameters changed without a version bump and a fresh validation run.

Stickiness and re-entry

A demotion cannot be undone by one good week. Re-promotion requires at least 40 new settled bets with a positive signal score over that fresh window. The fresh window restarts whenever a rule triggers, so a demoted strategy cannot inherit credit from the bets that got it demoted.
