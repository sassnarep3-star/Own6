The honest headline. The infrastructure the brief asks for now exists and is verified: a results feed that reconciles to official standings, a price store with real opens and closes, an origination order, a skill/staking split, a measured-correlation table with n, a replacement-depth chain and a demotion gate. The conclusion it produces is that nothing is certified yet: one settled game is not a sample. Every empty leaderboard on this site is a gate working, not a gap in the build.

Method in one paragraph

Scores come from the official weekly schedule pages and are checked by recomputing all 32 teams' records and points from the game rows and comparing them to the official standings — a single mismatch fails the build. Prices are the operator's own open and close fields, captured with a timestamp; a market is only gradeable if the provider had settled it. Fair lines are a declared, deliberately naive points-per-game model with every parameter listed and flagged, because fitting coefficients to this sample would produce confidence without evidence. Correlations are computed only from score data and carry n, a 95% interval and a p-value; when the input does not exist the pair is published as uncomputable rather than filled in.

Sources are linked on every row. A blank cell is a cell that no official source confirmed; it is never a zero and never an estimate. This pack is informational and non-commercial. It is not betting advice, and it makes no profitability claim.
