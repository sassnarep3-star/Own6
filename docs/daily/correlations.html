Measured dependence, and the cells we refuse to fill

Correlation is what decides whether a parlay is priced correctly. Multiplying marginal probabilities is only valid under independence; where the data show dependence, the joint probability has to be modelled. The first three rows are measured from the 121 official final scores in this repository. The prop rows are blank because no official, licensed player-level source is available to this project.

What a parlay builder should do with this. Two legs on the same game share a pace factor (row 2), so a joint model is required; two legs on different games can be treated as independent only after checking that no shared team or player appears in both, and even then the independence assumption is a modelling choice, not a measured fact.

Multiple testing. Three correlations are reported here; at a 5% level, roughly one false positive is expected per twenty tests. Nothing on this page is corrected for multiplicity because nothing here is being promoted as a signal — they are descriptions used to design the joint model.
