Eight gates, and a leaderboard that stays empty

Every candidate strategy has to clear the same gates before it can earn a leaderboard slot or a reward. A gate is only marked pass when the repository can show the evidence; the rights and market gates fail today for structural reasons, which is what keeps the leaderboard at zero qualified entries.

Reward design. Rewards are research priority and publication space, not money. Eligibility requires all eight gates; a candidate that fails any gate stays off the leaderboard no matter how good its point estimate looks. This is the mechanism that keeps sample-size luck from being rewarded.
