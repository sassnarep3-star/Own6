The one-line answer to "can we bet this yet". No. The frozen model's best backtest skill is +0.013 with a 95% week-cluster interval of -0.175 to +0.152 — it includes zero, so the model cannot be told apart from guessing the base rate. Of the 150-game corpus it could honestly score 134; the other 16 would have needed a same-week number and are published as refused, not dropped. On top of that no official page publishes a price, so no residual, ROI or CLV can be computed at all. The pack therefore publishes what it can prove and blanks the rest.
The one-line answer to "can we measure replacement depth yet". Partly, and better than before. The route that every earlier pack recorded as blocked — the official Game Book PDF, reachable or not — is reachable from this session. Four books were read end to end: 176 named starters, 50 Not Active entries, and the NFL's own playtime table. That table is captioned (Unofficial) by the NFL, so every snap value here is labelled Unofficial and none is called certified.
