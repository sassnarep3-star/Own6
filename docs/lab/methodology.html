Methodology

Uncertainty

ROI, CLV and predictive-quality improvements use a percentile bootstrap that resamples whole event clusters. Serial dependence across dates is not eliminated. The binomial tail is a diagnostic only; parlays and same-game props are clustered and heterogeneous.

Multiplicity

The Bonferroni adjustment is applied across all six frozen candidates, including abandoned ones. No adaptive search may add a new candidate after seeing holdout results.

What is not here

  • No synthetic or fabricated returns.
  • No sportsbook odds or closing prices.
  • No NBA statistics beyond the official glossary and schedule metadata that are directly linked above.
