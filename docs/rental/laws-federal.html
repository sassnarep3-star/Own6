Federal Law & HUD

There is no federal rent control. What exists instead: the Fair Housing Act (terms, conditions and privileges of rental), HUD's Fair Market Rents as program benchmarks, and the FHEO complaint path. FMR dollar figures are blank here — see F-R2.

No federal rent control exists. The federal government does not cap private rents. Federal law reaches this analysis in exactly two ways: (1) the Fair Housing Act bars discriminatory terms, conditions and privileges of rental — including how increases are set and served; (2) HUD Fair Market Rents are a federal benchmark for subsidized programs — not a cap, and not evidence of lawful rent for a specific unit.
