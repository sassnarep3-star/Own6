What anniversary means

Per SF.gov: first increase allowed 12 months after lease began; thereafter anniversary is date of last increase. If you move the date forward (e.g., Feb 1 → Apr 1 for Unit 1), any banked year not yet 12 months old is permanently lost. Verify Unit 1’s April 1 2026 continuing tenancy did not create a new anniversary before relying on bank.

Why these units are rent-controlled

Building 1940 predates June 13 1979 → fully covered under SF Admin Code Chapter 37 for multi-unit non-exempt structures. Single-family/condo Costa-Hawkins exemption (Civ 1954.50) does not apply to your 2-unit pre-1979 building while occupied. Check coverage via SF DBI Property Map + SF.gov rental-law screen.
