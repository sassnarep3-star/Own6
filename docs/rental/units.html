  • New rent$4,114.85

Assumptions & computation notes

  • No rent increase has been imposed on this tenancy since February 1, 2025 (rent unchanged at $3,995.00 through April 1, 2026).
  • Banked 1.4% requires at least 24 full months without any rent increase -> earliest on/after February 1, 2027 (2 years from Feb 1, 2025 tenancy start). See Flag F-R5.
  • Notice uses the percentage in effect on the effective date; banked part = $55.93; annual part = $63.92. Total $119.85.
  • Rent increases cannot be rounded up to the nearest dollar.
  • Imposing the banked increase sets a new anniversary date equal to the effective date.

U2-B — All skipped years banked + 2026-27 annual increase (impose in one notice)

  • Rate applied1.8% (6/1/20) + 0.7% (6/1/21) + 2.3% (6/1/22) + 3.6% (6/1/23) + 1.7% (6/1/24) + 1.4% (6/1/25) banked = 11.5%, + 1.6% (2026-27) annual = 13.1% combined
  • Dollar increase+$412.65
  • New rent$3,562.65
  • Notice90-DAY written notice REQUIRED (combined increase > 10% over the prior 12 months), Civil Code §827(b)(3)(A); notice must state which part is banked; +5 days if served by mail

Assumptions & computation notes

  • UNVERIFIED base rent (Flag F-R7): $3,150.00 assumed.
  • No increase ever imposed since June 1, 2019; banking has been allowed since April 1, 1982; banked increases do not expire during the tenancy and carry over to new owners but not new tenants (SF.gov).
  • Banked percentages are added and multiplied by the CURRENT base rent; they are not compounded or prorated (SF.gov).
  • 11.5% banked portion = $362.25; 1.6% annual portion = $50.40; total $412.65.
  • Combined single-notice increase of 13.1% exceeds 10%, so a 90-day notice is mandatory. Splitting into smaller annual notices, each ≤10% combined, would only allow 30-day notices but cannot lawfully exceed the AGA for any anniversary.
