F-R1highUnit 2 single-notice combined increase would exceed 10% — 90-day notice mandatory
If the owner imposed all banked increases (11.5%) plus the 2026-27 annual increase (1.6%) in one notice, the combined 13.1% increase exceeds 10% of the rent charged during the prior 12 months. California Civil Code §827(b)(3)(A) then requires at least 90 days' written notice (not 30). A 30-day notice would be defective. See S-12.
Action: Serve 90-day written notice, or phase increases across anniversaries (each ≤10% combined with proper notice), within AGA limits.		sources ↗

F-R4mediumWhether Unit 1's $3,995 February 2025 rent was a lawful vacancy-decontrol market reset is unverified
Costa-Hawkins (Civil Code §1954.53) allows an owner to set the initial rent on a NEW tenancy; SF imposes no vacancy-control cap on the first rent of an emptied unit (S-01, S-15). The records do not show whether the February 1, 2025 commencement was a fresh tenancy following a voluntary vacancy, or a re-papering of an existing tenancy (which could NOT lawfully reset rent). If the prior tenancy was terminated by the owner, exceptions may apply (§1954.53(a)(1)).
Action: Confirm with the prior-tenancy file; if it was an owner-terminated tenancy, consult Rent Board/counsel before treating $3,995 as freely set.		sources ↗
F-R5highUnit 1's skipped 2025-26 (1.4%) increase is NOT yet bankable
SF.gov: a skipped increase is banked only once at least 24 full months pass without any rent increase. The tenancy began February 1, 2025 and no increase has been imposed; 24 full months elapse on February 1, 2027. Imposing a 'banked' 1.4% before February 1, 2027 would exceed the lawful amount for the notice date. (The 2026-27 1.6% annual increase remains available on/after March 1, 2026 with a 12-month-anniversary-compliant notice.) See S-04.
Action: Do not impound any banked increase until on/after February 1, 2027; date the Unit 1 increase notice accordingly.		sources ↗

F-R10infoMail service adds 5 days (reconciled sources)
SF.gov pages state a mailed notice requires 5 more days (S-01, S-04). Civil Code §827(b)(2)–(3) incorporates CCP §1013 for mailed service (5 days within California). Consistent; the longer effective date governs. Personal delivery is the cleanest service method.

F-R11infoNot legal advice; SF tenant protections are strict and tenants get free legal help
Any increase is scrutinized against Chapter 37, Civil Code §§827, 1946.2, 1947.12, 1954.53, FEHA §12955, Unruh §51 and the Fair Housing Act.
