One Unit vs Both — Discrimination Analysis

Short answer: You may increase one unit only with neutral anniversary/bank basis, but raising both on same methodology on their own anniversaries is markedly safer in SF’s tenant-counsel environment. See 42 USC 3601-19 overview.

Why consistent methodology wins review

  • SF tenants have free counsel: Tenant Right to Counsel + BayLegal + EDC will test any selective increase for pretext.
  • Fair Housing prohibits different terms based on race/color/religion/sex/familial status/disability/national origin — includes disparate impact of facial neutral policy per CRS R48113 (inclusive communities 2015).
  • Retaliation presumption within 180 days of complaint per Civ 1942.5 — separate from discrimination but often examined together.

Written policy to keep on file: “All fully-covered units receive the annual allowable percentage on their anniversary; banked amounts added consistently where documented; no protected-class consideration; license and notice verified.”
