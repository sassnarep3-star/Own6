Legal framework · State of California

State Law — California

Costa-Hawkins vacancy decontrol, the Tenant Protection Act (AB 1482) and its exemptions, the §827 30/90-day notice rule, just cause (§1946.2), and Unruh/FEHA anti-discrimination statutes — each row linked to leginfo.legislature.ca.gov.

Layer order. For these two units the local AGA is the operative cap: AB 1482 yields to more-restrictive local rent control. State law still governs notice mechanics (§827), anti-discrimination (Unruh §51; FEHA §12955) and vacancy decontrol on new tenancies (Costa-Hawkins §1954.53).

AB 1482 quick-math (for the record)

If AB 1482 applied (it does not for these units): max annual increase = 5% + Bay Area CPI-U change, or 10%, whichever is lower, of the lowest rent charged in the prior 12 months; at most two increments per 12 months; clear written notice under Civil Code §827 still required. For SF rent-controlled units the operative number is the AGA (1.6% for 3/1/26–2/28/27), which is always tighter. (§1947.12(a)(1)–(2), (d)(3) — leginfo ↗)
