2 · What a "reasonable and normal" increase is here (verified)

The SF Rent Board sets the allowable increase each March at 60% of the Bay Area CPI-U change, capped at 7%, once per 12 months, base rent only. Building stock pre-June 13, 1979 ⇒ full coverage. AB 1482's 5%+CPI/10% cap is inapplicable (more-restrictive local law exemption, §1947.12(d)(3)). There is no federal rent control.

4 · Corporations vs individual owners

Same caps, same banking rules, same license and petition regime for every owner — what differs is practice discipline and one narrow AB 1482 carve-out (single-family/condo exemption is lost for REIT/corporation/LLC owners, §1947.12(d)(5)). Institutional owners typically run a calendar of maximum lawful AGAs and timely Housing Inventory filings; individual owners more often skip years (as on this property), banking value that may later be imposed once per tenancy with correct notice. No official dataset on increase behavior in 94122 could be read this session; market-practice observations are labeled and flagged (F-R13), not published as fact.

5 · Correct notification and reasonable reasons

Reasonable: cost of living (AGA); banked skips (≥24-month rule); petition-approved passthroughs (capital improvement, operating & maintenance, special circumstances, utility worksheet, bond passthroughs); vacancy decontrol on a genuinely new tenancy — nothing else. Unlawful: roommate surcharge; market-rate reset mid-tenancy; retaliation; any protected-class basis; increases without license/notice. Full notice procedure →

6 · One unit or both?

Both — using the same objective, published formula applied to each unit's own lawful base, with identical notice machinery and documentation. The law permits per-tenancy increases and does not require equality; but uniform treatment is the posture that cannot reasonably support a discrimination claim, mirrors "normal business practice," and survives tenant-lawyer scrutiny. Any single-unit raise needs a documented, objective reason (e.g., different banking histories) and carries elevated risk. Full analysis →

7 · Will it survive tenant-lawyer scrutiny?

Only if computed exactly as shown, licensed, and noticed correctly — and the Unit 2 ledger confirms the $3,150 base (F-R7), Unit 1's banked 1.4% waits until February 1, 2027 (F-R5), and Unit 2's combined 13.1% uses a 90-day notice (F-R1).
