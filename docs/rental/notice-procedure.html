Notice Procedure — How to Do It Right

The exact sequence SF.gov and Civil Code §827 require — license first, base-rent math, 30/90-day service rules, the mandatory notice contents, and what never qualifies as a lawful reason.

Golden rule. A rent increase that is substantively lawful can still be defeated by defective service. The sequence below is the one SF.gov and Civil Code §827 require; deviating converts a valid increase into an invalid one.

Step-by-step

  1. Confirm coverage & license. Both units: pre-6/13/1979 building ⇒ full Chapter 37 protection. Confirm the Rent Board Housing Inventory report is filed and the Rent Increase License is held before any allowable/banked increase may be imposed (in effect 7/1/2022; 3/1/2023 for condos & 1–9-unit buildings). SF.gov ↗
  2. Pull the ledger. Rent actually charged for the prior 12 months (for the §827 10% computation), every increase date since tenancy start, and every skipped year (for banking). Unit 2's ledger is not yet pulled — F-R7.
  3. Compute on base rent only. Base rent includes parking/storage housing services; excludes temporary passthroughs and fluctuating charges. 1.6% × $3,995.00 = $63.92 (Unit 1) / 1.6% × $3,150.00 = $50.40 (Unit 2). Never round up. SF.gov ↗
  4. Choose the increase size. One 12-month period per increase; no more than two increments/12 months under AB 1482 were it to apply (it does not). Combined increase in any 12-month window ≤10% ⇒ 30-day notice; >10% ⇒ 90-day.
  5. Determine the percentage for the notice. Use the AGA in effect on the effective date of the increase (not the date the notice is served). For 2026-27: 1.6%. SF.gov ↗
  6. Draft the notice. Must state: (a) the new rent in dollars; (b) the percentage; (c) the effective date; (d) if any portion is banked, which portion is banked. Identify the unit, the tenancy, and the signature/date. SF.gov banking ↗
  7. Serve correctly. Personal delivery to the tenant, or mail under CCP §1013 procedures (+5 days for California-mailed service). SF.gov: "30-day written notice / 90-day if >10% / +5 more days if mailed." Keep proof of service (declaration). CC §827 ↗
  8. Timing. First-ever increase: ≥12 months after the lease began. After any increase: ≥12 months before the next. Imposing late moves the anniversary date to the actual effective date (new anniversary). Unit 1: first increase eligible February 1, 2026 (2025-26, 1.4%) — skipped, so subject to the ≥24-month rule for banking; 2026-27 1.6% available now.
  9. Petition first? If any amount relies on capital improvement, operating & maintenance, special circumstances, utility worksheet or bond passthrough, file the petition/worksheet BEFORE serving the notice; amount is retroactive to the effective date in a valid notice only if approved. SF.gov petitions ↗
  10. After service. Keep a copy + proof; diarize the next eligible date; do not re-notice within 12 months; adjust the security deposit interest if deposits are involved (4.2% 2026-27, S-02).

Reasonable reasons — and what is never a reason

Reasonable, lawful reasons

  • Cost of living — the AGA (60% of Bay Area CPI-U, 1.6% now) (S-01 ↗)
  • Banked skipped increases — computed, noticed as banked portion (S-04 ↗)
  • Petition-approved: capital improvement; operating & maintenance; special circumstances / comparable units; Prop I past-history; utility; §1.21 occupancy (S-05 ↗)
  • Utility passthroughs on filed worksheet; GO-bond / water revenue bond passthroughs on worksheet (S-05 ↗)
  • Costa-Hawkins vacancy reset — NEW tenancy only, subject to §1954.53 exceptions (S-15 ↗)

Never a lawful reason

  • "Market rent" above the AGA during an ongoing tenancy
  • New or additional roommate (expressly unlawful even if the lease allows a surcharge) (S-01 ↗)
  • Retaliation for protected activity — complaints, organizing, asserting habitability rights (FEHA §12955(f); see S-17 ↗)
  • Any protected-class basis — race, color, religion, sex, gender, gender identity/expression, sexual orientation, marital status, national origin, ancestry, familial status, source of income, disability, veteran/military status, genetic information (S-17 ↗ · S-16 ↗ · S-18 ↗)
  • Lack of license, wrong notice period, math errors, rounding up — procedural, not substantive
