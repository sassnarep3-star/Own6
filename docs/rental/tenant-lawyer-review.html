Tenant-Lawyer Review — SF Scrutiny Checklist

How a San Francisco tenant advocate (or the Rent Board, or CRD/HUD) attacks an increase — row by row — with the evidence this property can produce today, and the free legal help both tenant cohorts can access.

Why this matters. San Francisco tenants facing eviction have a right to a free attorney (Tenant Right to Counsel — Prop F, 2018), and every tenant can get free counseling from the Rent Board. An increase that would not survive a tenant lawyer's review is an increase destined for a Rent Board petition, a complaint to CRD (FEHA) or HUD (FHA), or litigation. Design to pass.
