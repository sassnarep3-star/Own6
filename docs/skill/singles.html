Reading

The model's paired loss advantage is negative on average and its interval spans zero in both the analytic and the block-bootstrap form. That is consistent with no predictive advantage over the de-vigged market on this slate. The 7–8 and 8–7 lean records give exact two-sided binomial p-values of 1.00 and 1.00 — the least surprising result in the document, and exactly what a coin would produce.

Fifteen bets cannot separate skill from luck at any plausible edge size (see the power table). The slate is reported because it exists, not because it is informative.
