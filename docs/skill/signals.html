Why most rows say UNTESTABLE

A signal is only testable if you hold the input as of before the bet and the price you would actually have received. The repository has results (92 games, each linked to its official NBA game page) and one slate of prices. It has no archive of quotes across time, which is the one thing that makes closing-line value measurable.

The NBA's own terms are the binding constraint, not the statistics. §9 of the NBA Terms of Use restricts use of NBA Statistics in connection with gambling activity, and the NBA Stats FAQ states that statistics are not available for download. This section therefore publishes no scraped NBA dataset and no invented price.

What would unblock each family

Rights first. A written permission or a licensed provider contract that covers gambling-related use would turn SG01 from a fail into a pass and would let the four UNTESTABLE price-dependent families be evaluated. Until then they stay listed and unmeasured rather than quietly dropped — dropping them would inflate every multiple-testing correction in our favour.
