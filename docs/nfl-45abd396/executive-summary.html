Executive summary

What changed since the last capture

  • Week 10 is now settled on the official schedule page: 13 of 14 cards print FINAL. The previous capture (2026-11-15/16) had 5 of 14 unsettled, which is why no leaderboard score could be published.
  • For the first time the repo's pre-kickoff Week-10 forecasts could be scored: 48 settled rows across five strategies, each traced to the commit that published it and checked against that fixture's kickoff.
  • Fourteen forecast rows were committed after their game kicked off and are labelled post_kickoff; they are excluded from every score. Publishing that label is the difference between a backtest and a fantasy.
  • Ten more official pages were read for availability, roster and venue facts: 347 injury rows across 28 clubs, 25 reserve/injured entries, 12 November trades, 13 Week-11 fixtures.

What the numbers say

  • Every strategy has a positive point estimate of Brier skill against the same-games climatology. Best is WK-ML at +0.4120 (Brier 0.130667 vs 0.222222); weakest is N6F-ML at +0.1962.
  • None of the five is qualified. Every 95% game-cluster interval contains zero at n = 12. The strategies forecast the same twelve games, so their agreement is not independent confirmation.
  • Three strategies hit 11 of 12 against a coin's expectation of 6. That is unusual under coin-flipping (p ≈ 0.003) and still not evidence of skill, because twelve games is one draw and the multiplicity adjustment over five strategies is 0.01021.
  • Strategy disagreement is small: a mean gap of 0.147 in home win probability. They share one input (PF/PA per game), so they are not five opinions.

What it does not mean

  • No profit claim is possible. NFL.com publishes no price, so there is no market line, no residual, no closing-line value and no ROI anywhere on this site — not because they were hard, but because the data does not exist without a licence (Q-02).
  • No injury signal is claimed. Zero of 155 paired designations moved between the available reads, because both reads came after the games. The delta is zero for a reason that is about timing, not about football.
  • No replacement is named. The weekly report publishes a designation, not a depth chart. Every "replacement" and "actual starter" cell is blank and flagged.

What would change the conclusion

  • A game-cluster lower bound above zero after the multiplicity adjustment — roughly 60–120 settled games at the observed variance, i.e. four to eight more weeks.
  • A licensed price archive, which would let the same probabilities be scored on CLV and residual-vs-market instead of Brier alone.
  • Game Books or an official snap-count table, which would turn the availability graph from expected-only into expected-vs-actual and make a personnel ablation possible.
  • A single pre-declared ablation pair for any availability feature, scored on a holdout never used for selection — the only way "injuries matter by X points" becomes falsifiable.

The three things to do next, in order

  2. Answer Q-01 (rights). Nothing scheduled — the Wed/Thu/Fri injury cadence, the price archive, the daily depth sweep — can start until that is answered in writing.
  3. Read the 13 Week-11 game-centre Game Info blocks to finish the venue register, which is the cheapest remaining gate that is blocked only by effort rather than by rights.
