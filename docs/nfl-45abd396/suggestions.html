How to choose

  • S-08 first — it costs nothing and stops the site from ever reading as approval.
  • S-02 and S-03 next — they make every later result harder to over-read.
  • S-07 before adding any new pack — the duplicate-table problem grows faster than the data does.
  • S-09 last — an ablation cannot be run until Q-08 and Q-06 are closed.
