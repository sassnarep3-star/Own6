Federal and state limits in context

Federal Fair Housing law is not itself a general rent-control percentage schedule. State Civil Code §1947.12 is a statewide rent cap with defined applicability and exemptions. Do not assume a 1940 construction date alone answers state-law coverage or SF local rent-control status; seek a determination based on the specific property and landlord facts. San Francisco’s published annual allowance and procedural prerequisites remain central to the user-reported controlled tenancies.

How to verify

Review current SF Rent Board annual allowance, rent increase license and Housing Inventory records; retrieve all rent notices and receipts; confirm tenancy status in writing; and obtain advice from the Rent Board or a qualified California landlord-tenant attorney. No market-comparable rent data is used: it would not independently authorize a controlled-rent increase.
