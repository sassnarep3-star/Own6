THE MATH, VERIFIED

Calculations — worked line by line

Rules first, then the two worked official examples, then our units. All dollar figures are computations labeled as such; the law is cited, not restated from memory.

The rules (as published by the Rent Board)

  1. Multiply current lawful base rent by the AGA — 2026-27 = 1.6%. Parking/storage included in base rent count; temporary passthroughs do not [S01].
  2. No rounding up to the nearest dollar — $63.92 stays $63.92 [S01].
  3. Once per 12 months; the first increase is due no sooner than 12 months after the tenancy began; a late increase moves the anniversary date [S01].
  4. Banking: an unused AGA banks only if ≥ 24 full months pass with no increase; banked amounts never compound — add the percentages, then multiply by the current base rent once; no cap on the total banked; does not expire during the tenancy; does not carry to a new tenant [S04].
  5. Use the AGA in effect on the effective date of the increase, not the date notice is served [S01].
  6. Notice: 30 days ≤10%; 90 days >10%; +5 days if mailed [S01][S09].
  7. The increase is void in practice if the landlord lacks a rent increase license (Housing Inventory) on the effective date [S06].

Official worked examples (verbatim method, Rent Board)

Example 1 — multiple-year banking, from S01

Tenant moved in 2023-06-01 at $1,000.00; first increase 2026-06-01. Banked: 2024 (1.7%) + 2025 (1.4%) = 3.1%; plus current 2026 (1.6%) = 4.7%. New base rent = $1,047.00. (The 2025 1.4% is usable only once ≥24 full months have elapsed since it became available; imposing on 2026-06-01 via the anniversary satisfies that rule for the 2024 increment; the Rent Board’s own example nonetheless yields 4.7% — edge mechanics are fact-specific; confirm with the counselor.)

Example 2 — banking, from S04

Tenant moved in 2022-08-01 at $1,300.00; first increase 2025-08-01. Skipped 2023 (3.6%) and 2024 (1.7%) → 5.3% banked. Plus 2025-08-01 annual 1.4% → 6.7% total. $1,300.00 × 6.7% = $87.10 → new base rent $1,387.10. Banked increments added, never compounded.
