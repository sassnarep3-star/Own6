THE LAW THAT APPLIES

Local · state · federal — what governs these units

ZIP 94122 lies entirely within the City and County of San Francisco; rent-control coverage is citywide — there is no 94122-specific rate. For these pre-1979 units the order of precedence is: SF Rent Ordinance (strictest, governs) → excluded from the state cap → no federal rent cap at all.
