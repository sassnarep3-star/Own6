HOW TO NOTIFY TENANTS

Notice & process — the exact steps

Pre-flight checklist (do these before drafting anything)

  1. Confirm coverage & exemptions — multi-unit, pre-6/13/1979, not SFH/condo-exempt, not fully exempt (laws); if unsure, call the Rent Board counselor 415-252-4600 [S05].
  2. File the Housing Inventory (or confirm this year’s filing) at portal.sfrb.org by March 1 — this generates the rent increase license required for the increase to take effect [S06].
  3. Pull the rent ledger — confirm the current lawful base rent and that no increase took effect within the last 12 months (once-per-12-month rule) [S01].
  4. Confirm the anniversary date — 2025-02-01 (Unit 1) / 2019-06-01 (Unit 2), or the date of the last increase if later [S01].
  5. Check the 180-day retaliation window — any habitability complaint, repair request registered with an agency, or tenant organizing? If yes, an increase today is presumptively retaliatory — do not proceed [S10].
  6. Choose the AGA in effect on the effective date (1.6% through 2027-02-28; 0% cannot be imposed; 2027-28 rate announced ~Jan 2027) [S01][F-07].

Illustrative notice template (label: illustrative draft — not legal advice; have counsel review)

NOTICE OF RENT INCREASE
To: The tenants of Unit __ (address withheld — internal record only)
Date: ____________

The landlord hereby gives notice that, beginning on ____________ , the base rent
for the unit will increase from $____ to $____ , an increase of $____ ( __.__% ).

[Banked portion, if any: $____ ( __.__% ) of this increase reflects annual
allowable increases for the following periods that were not previously imposed:
____________________________________________________ .
The remaining $____ ( __.__% ) is the annual allowable increase in effect for
the effective date above.]

This increase takes effect on the date above, which is the tenant’s anniversary
date for purposes of the San Francisco Residential Rent Stabilization and
Arbitration Ordinance. It is the only increase for the 12-month period
beginning on that date.

Rent increases are calculated on the lawful base rent only and are not rounded
up. The landlord holds a current rent increase license issued through the SF
Rent Board Housing Inventory as of the effective date.

Service: served personally / by mail on ____________ .
(If mailed: this notice is deemed served five (5) days after mailing per
CCP § 1013.)

If a tenant pushes back

  • Tenant may file a Rent Board petition challenging the increase (unlawful amount, missing license, incorrect notice). The Rent Board can strike or reduce the increase; the tenant does not need to move out first. — Rent Board, general framework; see counselor contact above [S05].
  • Free tenant-side help exists in SF and California: SF Rent Board counselor 415-252-4600 (free, weekdays) [S05]; statewide legal-aid referral LawHelpCA (host referenced by the CA Attorney General; non-official host, labeled) [S17]; CRD complaint channel for discrimination [S12]; HUD FHEO complaint (federal) [S11][S18].
  • Document everything: ledger, license confirmation, notice copy, proof of service. Disputes are won on records, not recollections.
