Method, stated plainly

  • Read path. Pages were read with the session's page-fetch tool over HTTPS against the host named in each row — www.nfl.com and three club domains, no third-party aggregators, no cached mirrors, and no proxy hosts (a proxy-fetch attempt this session returned an access-denied page and was discarded; nothing in this pack came from it).
  • Transcription path. The tool returns the page's text; the session wrote what it saw into data/nfl_2dd0cbcb/raw/*.md, including table rows that were inconvenient (blank cells, contradictory headings). Every CSV in this pack is derived from those files by scripts/build_nfl_2dd0cbcb_data.py, offline — there is no hand-typed number in the derived tables, and CI re-runs the derivation and fails if the output drifts.
  • Clock path. read_on_utc is the artifact-write time, an upper bound on the fetch. Where two reads share one date (11/19 morning and 11/19 midday), both are kept as separate rows — a zero-change result across the two is published as a zero, because "the designations did not move" is itself the finding.
  • Nothing inferred. No starter is inferred from a depth chart, no injury from a blank cell, no market price from a model line, no venue from a marketing param. Those four rules account for most of the blank cells on this site.
