Standing rules this pack follows

  • No market prices. No official page read by any session carries a market price, so price columns stay blank by written policy — not by oversight, and not filled with a model output relabelled as a market line.
  • No profit or return claims. Nothing on this site is priced, and the release gate bans profit vocabulary; the leaderboard's honest current answer is that no strategy has qualified, which is the scoreboard working rather than failing.
  • Blank beats invented. Where the official record is contradictory (venue names, schedule headings, the Arizona quarterback room), both readings are published verbatim and the contradiction is flagged. Reconciliation is an owner decision, not a build step.
  • needs review never blocks a merge, and a flag here never edits another pack's files: requests against other sessions' registers are logged as rows in this pack's queue.
