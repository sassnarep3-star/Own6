Key findings

  1. Legal exposure is the main risk. Under Art. 26(2), designing or providing a betting-type system, providing game information for it, or promoting or brokering it is prohibited, and so is paying benefits to winners of similar issuance under Art. 26(1). Penalties run up to 7 years (Art. 47). Bettors can also be punished (Art. 48(3)). Originating NBA lines and running a reward leaderboard for Korean users is the highest-risk combination. This is a reading of the text, not a legal opinion (flag F01).
  2. The gates are designed, not passed. 8 validation gates are defined; 0 have passed. Luck cannot be removed from the equation. The gates only reduce the chance of confusing luck with skill (see Methodology).
  3. The NBA's restrictions are real but not yet primary-sourced. The official release confirms restrictions exist. The specific bans need the NBA's own memo to be confirmed (flag F03).
  4. Repo state. The repo was a one-line README. This work adds data, a site, and coordination records. Irregularities are listed on Flagged for review.

Decisions needed from the owner

  1. Get a written opinion from Korean counsel on Art. 26 before any public output or reward scheme (G1).
  2. Choose a verified odds and results source with documented terms, or accept that strategy work stays empty (G2).
  3. Decide whether the site stays research-only. Current design: no betting recommendations, no rewards configured.
