# Laws and Regulations

## Local Law (San Francisco Rent Ordinance)
- **Applicable Cap**: 1.6% for increases effective March 1, 2026 through February 28, 2027 [SF.gov](https://www.sf.gov/news--annual-rent-increase-3126-22827-announced) [SF.gov Learn](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco?_gl=1*1aixd12*_ga*MTEwODA2MzMyMS4xNzA2ODkwMTk0*_ga_BT9NDE0NFC*MTcwNjg5MDE5NC4xLjAuMTcwNjg5MDE5Ni4wLjAuMA..) [SF.gov Reports](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit)
- **Coverage**: Units with certificate of occupancy on or before June 13, 1979 (both units built in 1940 qualify) [RentReboot](https://rentreboot.com/guide/bay-area-rent-control-by-city-2026) [Vanderburgh](https://www.vanderburghhouse.com/rent-control-sober-living-san-francisco/)
- **Calculation**: 60% of the change in the Consumer Price Index (CPI) for the San Francisco-Oakland metropolitan area [SF.gov Learn](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco?_gl=1*1aixd12*_ga*MTEwODA2MzMyMS4xNzA2ODkwMTk0*_ga_BT9NDE0NFC*MTcwNjg5MDE5NC4xLjAuMTcwNjg5MDE5Ni4wLjAuMA..) [SF.gov Reports](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit)
- **Frequency**: One increase per twelve months; unused increases may be banked and combined later [SF.gov Learn](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco?_gl=1*1aixd12*_ga*MTEwODA2MzMyMS4xNzA2ODkwMTk0*_ga_BT9NDE0NFC*MTcwNjg5MDE5NC4xLjAuMTcwNjg5MDE5Ni4wLjAuMA..) [StructureProperties](https://www.structureproperties.com/locations/san-francisco/)

## State Law (California Tenant Protection Act AB 1482)
- **Applicable Cap**: 8.8% for August 1, 2026 through July 31, 2027 (5% + 3.8% regional CPI) [CoastlineEquity](https://coastlineequity.net/insights/california-rent-increase-caps-2026-2027-guide) [SF.gov AB1482](https://www.sf.gov/reports--california-tenant-protection-act-2019-ab-1482)
- **Coverage**: Units NOT covered by local rent control (e.g., single-family homes, condos, or buildings with certificate of occupancy after June 13, 1979) [SF.gov AB1482](https://www.sf.gov/reports--california-tenant-protection-act-2019-ab-1482) [CoastlineEquity](https://coastlineequity.net/insights/california-rent-increase-caps-2026-2027-guide)
- **Note**: Local ordinance (1.6%) overrides state cap (8.8%) for covered units [SF.gov AB1482](https://www.sf.gov/reports--california-tenant-protection-act-2019-ab-1482) [CoastlineEquity](https://coastlineequity.net/insights/california-rent-increase-caps-2026-2027-guide)

## Federal Law
- **Federal Level**: No specific federal rental increase cap law exists; federal laws (e.g., Fair Housing Act) prohibit discrimination but do not set percentage caps [HUD Guidelines]
- **Interaction**: Federal laws govern discrimination and accessibility; local/state laws govern percentage caps [HUD]
