# Corporate vs Individual Property Owner Practices

## Big Real Estate Corporations
- **Practice**: Corporations (e.g., Trinity, Equity Residential, Veritas, Mosser) often use algorithms or software (e.g., RealPage) to track increases and bank unused allowances [SFChronicle](https://www.sfchronicle.com/bayarea/article/sf-biggest-residential-landlords-22442815.php) [KQED](https://www.kqed.org/news/11995878/ai-raising-the-rent-san-francisco-could-be-the-first-city-to-ban-the-practice)
- **Compliance**: Corporations track unit history, apply increases on schedule, and bank unused increases (e.g., 1.6% annually) [StructureProperties](https://www.structureproperties.com/locations/san-francisco/)
- **Scrutiny**: Corporations face lawsuits for algorithmic collusion or price-fixing; thus, they strictly adhere to the 1.6% cap for covered units to avoid litigation [SFChronicle](https://www.sfchronicle.com/bayarea/article/sf-biggest-residential-landlords-22442815.php) [KQED](https://www.kqed.org/news/11995878/ai-raising-the-rent-san-francisco-could-be-the-first-city-to-ban-the-practice)

## Individual Property Owners
- **Practice**: Individuals (91.45% of SF landlords) often manage manually; may lose track of banked increases [Stanford Study](https://web.stanford.edu/~diamondr/RentControlPP.pdf)
- **Compliance**: Must follow the same 1.6% cap and notice requirements; often rely on SF Rent Board counseling [SF.gov Learn](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco?_gl=1*1aixd12*_ga*MTEwODA2MzMyMS4xNzA2ODkwMTk0*_ga_BT9NDE0NFC*MTcwNjg5MDE5NC4xLjAuMTcwNjg5MDE5Ni4wLjAuMA..) [StructureProperties](https://www.structureproperties.com/locations/san-francisco/)
- **Risk**: Individuals more likely to face scrutiny for improper notices or exceeding caps due to lack of automated tracking [Stanford Study](https://web.stanford.edu/~diamondr/RentControlPP.pdf)
