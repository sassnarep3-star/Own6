# Tenant Lawyer Scrutiny and Advocacy

## Official Sources for Review
- **SF Rent Board**: Provides free counseling and formal petition hearings; can review if increase exceeds 1.6% [StructureProperties](https://www.structureproperties.com/locations/san-francisco/) [WayThroughProject](https://waythroughproject.com/resources/cities/san-francisco)
- **Eviction Defense Collaborative (EDC)**: Free legal representation for tenants (Right to Counsel program) [GiampaoliLaw](https://giampaolilaw.com/resources/) [WayThroughProject](https://waythroughproject.com/resources/cities/san-francisco)
- **SF Tenants Union**: Counseling for tenants only [GiampaoliLaw](https://giampaolilaw.com/resources/)
- **Housing Rights Committee**: Counseling on rights [GiampaoliLaw](https://giampaolilaw.com/resources/)
- **Cause Justa**: Advocacy and information [GiampaoliLaw](https://giampaolilaw.com/resources/)
- **Bay Area Legal Aid**: Free legal aid for low-income tenants [WayThroughProject](https://waythroughproject.com/resources/cities/san-francisco)

## Scrutiny Criteria (Must Pass Review)
1. **Percentage Cap**: Must not exceed 1.6% for covered units (March 1, 2026 – February 28, 2027) [SF.gov News](https://www.sf.gov/news--annual-rent-increase-3126-22827-announced) [SF.gov Learn](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco?_gl=1*1aixd12*_ga*MTEwODA2MzMyMS4xNzA2ODkwMTk0*_ga_BT9NDE0NFC*MTcwNjg5MDE5NC4xLjAuMTcwNjg5MDE5Ni4wLjAuMA..)
2. **Notice Period**: 30 days for increases under 10% [Platuni](https://www.platuni.com/compliance/us/california/san-francisco-rent-increase-limit-2026-2027-1-6-percent) [Vanderburgh](https://www.vanderburghhouse.com/rent-control-sober-living-san-francisco/)
3. **Base Rent Calculation**: Must be on base rent only (no temporary passthroughs) [SF.gov Learn](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco?_gl=1*1aixd12*_ga*MTEwODA2MzMyMS4xNzA2ODkwMTk0*_ga_BT9NDE0NFC*MTcwNjg5MDE5NC4xLjAuMTcwNjg5MDE5Ni4wLjAuMA..)
4. **No Discrimination**: Must comply with Fair Housing Act (federal) and SF Ordinance [SF Rent Board]
5. **Banked Increases**: Only if previously unused and properly tracked [SF.gov Learn](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco?_gl=1*1aixd12*_ga*MTEwODA2MzMyMS4xNzA2ODkwMTk0*_ga_BT9NDE0NFC*MTcwNjg5MDE5NC4xLjAuMTcwNjg5MDE5Ni4wLjAuMA..) [StructureProperties](https://www.structureproperties.com/locations/san-francisco/)

## Lawyer Review Outcome
- **Normal Business Practice**: Applying exactly 1.6% with proper notice and license is considered normal and lawful [SF.gov News](https://www.sf.gov/news--annual-rent-increase-3126-22827-announced) [SF.gov Learn](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco?_gl=1*1aixd12*_ga*MTEwODA2MzMyMS4xNzA2ODkwMTk0*_ga_BT9NDE0NFC*MTcwNjg5MDE5NC4xLjAuMTcwNjg5MDE5Ni4wLjAuMA..)
- **Discrimination/Broken Laws**: Any increase above 1.6% without petition approval, or targeting specific tenants based on protected classes, would fail review [SF Rent Board / Fair Housing Act]
- **Advocate Position**: Tenant advocates (e.g., SF Tenants Union, EDC) scrutinize any increase; free legal aid available if increase is deemed excessive or discriminatory [GiampaoliLaw](https://giampaolilaw.com/resources/) [WayThroughProject](https://waythroughproject.com/resources/cities/san-francisco)
