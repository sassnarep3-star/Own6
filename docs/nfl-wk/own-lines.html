Originated lines: what our own numbers say, and what they deliberately do not

Every one of the 13 unplayed Week 10 games gets four numbers computed only from the official standings: a total, a home spread, a home win probability and a fair American price. The market columns are empty because no licensed price exists.

The method, in full

Inputs (official)

Each club's points-for per game and points-against per game from nfl.com/standings, plus the fixture from nfl.com Week 10 schedule. Nothing else.

Formulas

own_total = mean(hPF, hPA, aPF, aPA)
own_spread = ((hPF + aPA) - (aPF + hPA)) / 2
P(home win) = Phi(own_spread / 5.9044)
fair price = fair American odds for that probability, no margin

Deliberately omitted

No home-field advantage. The official standings do not publish a home-field parameter and inventing one would be fabrication. No opponent adjustment. Adjusting requires play-by-play or an efficiency feed that is not licensed here. No market comparison. No price exists.

What this means

Treat these as a transparent baseline, not a forecast. A model that ignores home field will systematically under-rate home clubs, and that bias is large enough to swamp any edge a naive comparison would show.

The one finished game, scored against these same formulas

Originated lines are descriptive, not forecasts, so this is not a backtest and one game proves nothing either way. It is here because a reader should see the model's error on the single game that already has a result before trusting any of the other thirteen. Commanders at Giants, 2026-11-12: Washington 24, New York Giants 21 (official schedule).

The spread was on the wrong side of the result. The formulas had the home club winning by 1.89; the home club lost by 3. The total was within 0.44 point. A single observation cannot separate bias from noise, but it does show the direction of the first miss, and it is why the model carries no home-field term: adding an invented home-field constant would have made this particular miss larger, not smaller.

Sanity checks a reviewer can run

  • The fair home and away moneylines must be consistent: their implied probabilities sum to 1.000 with no vig.
  • The own total should sit near the league average of 45.92 points per game; large deviations follow directly from the two clubs' rates.
  • A club with a negative points differential per game should receive a spread against it in most matchups.

All three checks are implemented in tests/test_nfl_wk.py and run in CI.
