A cascade is not a projection. It says the club's own page lists no available man at the top two slots; it says nothing about how the third man will play. Where the chart shows only two names, the pack records "not identifiable" rather than assuming a promotion from elsewhere on the roster.

The depth-deficiency method, stated so it can be attacked

  1. String is the 1-based position at which a club's own page lists a player, taking his best slot across the position groups he appears in. A player absent from the page has no string.
  2. Declared availability is a disclosed policy weight, not an official probability: Out = 0.00, Questionable = 0.50, no game status but full practice = 1.00, limited = 0.50, did not participate = 0.00. The league publishes the labels, not their probabilities, so these weights are ours.
  3. A deficiency event is a first-string player ruled Out while the club chart lists a replacement behind him. That is a count, not a measurement.
  4. What is not claimed: that the replacement is worse, by how much, or how many points it costs.
