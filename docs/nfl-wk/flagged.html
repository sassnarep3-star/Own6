The three you should look at first

  1. FLAG-01 — no licence. NFL Terms §1.3 restricts this use. Everything else is downstream of that.
  2. FLAG-02 — no market price. This is why there is no edge, no ROI and no ranking anywhere on the site.
  3. FLAG-04 — the official injury policy URL is dead. A source cited by earlier sessions returns a 404 today, so the 90-minute inactive deadline is not asserted here.

How blank cells are marked

A blank cell in a table is rendered with a hatched background and an accessible label of "blank, unverified". It is never rendered as a zero, a dash that could be read as zero, or an interpolated value.
