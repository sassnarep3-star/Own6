Suggestions: review, choose, then implement

This is the section you asked for: a place to read every proposed change, see what it costs and what it risks, and choose the best path before anything is built. The decision column is empty on purpose — it is yours to fill.

How to use this page. Each row is a separate, reversible proposal. Nothing in the "awaiting_decision" set has been implemented. The items marked "implemented" are already live on this site because they cost nothing and made the evidence clearer; they are still listed so you can reject and revert them. If you pick a row, the only thing needed is the value in decision owner needs.

The decision workflow

  1. Read the problem the suggestion solves. If you do not recognise the problem, the suggestion is not ready.
  2. Check the risk column. A high-impact change with an unmitigated risk should wait for its blocker.
  3. Note what the owner needs. Some rows need a budget, a licence, or agreement from another session.
  4. Write your decision in the empty column. Accept, reject or defer. Deferred is a real answer.
  5. Only then implement. An accepted suggestion becomes a task with its own gate in the validation register.
