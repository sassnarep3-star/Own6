Why every strategy reads "QUARANTINED"

Eligibility is computed, not typed. The gate engine in src/nfl_wk/gates.py returns PASS only when the named artefact exists in the repository. The gate that decides whether a price may be compared at all (VG-03) is blocked because no licensed archive exists, so every path that requires it fails closed. That is the intended behaviour: a competition that cannot verify a result does not get to award a rank.

Reward policy

Rewards are research units, not money. A unit buys a reviewer's attention. The tiers are defined in src/nfl_wk/gates.py and shown on the leaderboard page.

What would unblock this

  1. A written licence or consent covering analytics and database construction (Q-01).
  2. A timestamped closing-line and prop archive from a licensed provider (Q-02, Q-03).
  3. Paired outcome history for the correlation register (Q-11).
  4. A pre-registered effect size, so the sample floor is derived rather than asserted (Q-12).

Those four items are the whole critical path. Nothing else in this pack is blocking a real evaluation.
