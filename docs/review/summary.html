Interpretation

The framework separates skill (repeatable, out-of-sample, market-beating calibration and closing-line value) from luck (small-sample outcomes). No strategy is claimed profitable, and no reward scheme is asserted as empirically validated. See validation gates and leaderboard on the main dashboard, plus the methodology basis from scikit-learn and NIST.
