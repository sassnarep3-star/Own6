What the extra official columns add

The official games pages print in-season tournament group records next to each team, for example WAS 1-0 and ATL 0-0 in East Group C. Those values were not in the published slate file; they are recorded here as verified readings and are not used in the model. Dropping the competition’s title-sponsor prefix from the published label is noted on each affected row rather than treated as an error.

Independent recheck of values published in this repository. A match means the published value equals the value read on an official NBA page on 2026-11-05. It is not a data licence, not proof of a betting edge, and not betting advice. NBA Terms of Use §1 and §9 govern reuse: nba.com/termsofuse.

Values that could not be confirmed from an official source are left blank and listed on Flagged for Review. Computed rows are calculations over official final scores, not official NBA values.
