The NBA Terms of Use decide what may be done with NBA data at all. The clauses that matter for this project were read on 2026-11-05 and are reproduced verbatim, with the official page linked on every row.

§9(iv) bars gambling use of NBA Statistics, including legal gambling activity. §1 requires written permission before Basketball Content is reused or linked. No authorization has been obtained, so this project publishes a research framework and provenance record, not NBA statistics packaged as a betting product.
