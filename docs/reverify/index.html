What the recheck does not prove

No market price

No sportsbook line, closing price or de-vigged probability is available under an authorized feed. Line value, edge, ROI and CLV stay blank. The recheck cannot and does not compare the model against a price.

Skill interval still spans zero

On the 61-game holdout the win-probability Brier skill score is 0.1299 with a 95% bootstrap interval of -0.0385 to 0.2961. That is compatible with luck, so no strategy is promoted.

Equal-opportunity schedule

Teams have played unequal numbers of games at this point of the season, so rating precision differs by team. The interval-based line output reflects that; a single-point line estimate is not a claim of accuracy.

Reproduction is not independence

The holdout reproduction uses the same window and seed, so it proves the published numbers come from the committed data, not that a different analyst would reach the same conclusion.
