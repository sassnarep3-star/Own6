Computed layer

Derived numbers, recomputed independently

The published model, correlation and holdout statistics were recomputed with separately written code: a Cholesky solve instead of Gauss-Jordan, and raw-score sums instead of centred means. All 89 recomputed values reproduce the published ones.

These are calculations over official final scores. They are not official NBA values, and they are not evidence of an edge. Where a value depends on a price, it stays blank.
