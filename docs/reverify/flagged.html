Limitations of this recheck

  • A value that matches an official page today is a provenance fact, not a licence to reuse NBA data.
  • The holdout test has 61 games. It cannot separate skill from luck at conventional confidence, and the published interval says so.
  • Teams have played unequal numbers of games, so rating precision differs by team.
  • Recomputing with the same window and seed proves the published numbers come from the committed data; it is not an independent replication by a different analyst.
  • No legal opinion has been obtained on §1 or §9.
