The headline number

The originated side went 28-44 over 72 graded bets in weeks 5-9 (hit rate 0.3889, one-sided p vs a fair coin 0.9778). The model is not merely unproven; at this sample it is actively wrong on the side. The demotion gate correctly returns it to paper.

What would change the answer

  • An official, citable market line. Without one the difference column cannot exist.
  • A forward sample of 200+ bets, which is a matter of weeks, not analysis.
  • Depth charts for the remaining 30 clubs, so the depth signal is not a two-club anecdote.
