The two scores, defined

signal_score = evidence term (1 − √(p/0.5)) × sample term √(n/100), scaled to 100. It cannot be raised by betting more.

staking_score = 50 × ROI/0.20 clamped to ±50 at a declared flat stake and −110. It is blank for the moneyline because no moneyline price exists.

A divergence flag of positive_staking_masks_weak_signal is exactly the failure mode this table was built to expose.
