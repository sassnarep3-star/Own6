Decision questions

The questions that must be answered before any decision is reasonable, split into what we know, what we do not, and how to find out. Five of these need an owner choice; the rest need data collection or time.

DQ-01MarketDoes an official, citable NFL line exist for any market?NFL.com serves a 404 at /odds/. The terms and the betting-partner release contain no price.Whether any price exists behind a login, in NFL+ or in an operator feed.If no citable line exists, the difference column can never be filled, and no strategy can ever be shown to beat the market.Probe nfl.com sitemap and search for an odds product page; if none, ask the owner whether a licensed operator feed is acceptable and record the answer as a scope change.open
DQ-02DataIs the 2026 week structure (14 games, 4 byes in Week 10) correct, or a capture artefact?The by-week page lists 14 games plus a 'Teams on Bye' block naming CHI, DEN, PHI, TB.Whether NFL.com was mid-update when read, or the season genuinely has this shape.Sample size per week feeds every gate. A wrong week count changes n.Re-read the same page on a later date and diff; also read nfl.com/schedules/2026 (by team).open
DQ-03InjuriesWhich depth chart is authoritative when a club page and the injury report disagree?Club pages list a player who the official injury report rules Out (6 such cases).Which page the club treats as operative on game day.The replacement register's 'first available' answer depends on which page wins.Treat the injury report as authoritative for availability and the depth chart as authoritative for ordering; record that rule here and apply it mechanically. Done in src/nfl_market/depth.py.resolved_by_rule
DQ-04StakingWhat stake schedule should the ROI column assume?A flat 1-unit stake at -110 is used and labelled.Whether the owner bets flat, by edge, or by Kelly.ROI is meaningless without a stake rule; different rules rank strategies differently.Owner decision. Options in suggestions S-02.awaiting_owner_choice
DQ-05ModelShould the side model be scrapped, refitted, or kept as a control?It went 28-44 walking forward.Whether the failure is the rating method, the sample, or both.Every downstream strategy inherits this. Keeping it silently would poison the catalog.Options in suggestions S-01. Until decided, the side is published with its record attached.awaiting_owner_choice
DQ-06ValidationWhat counts as a 'forward' bet for gate G09?The gate requires 200 out-of-sample bets after the gate set is frozen.Whether a paper bet placed after the freeze but never staked counts.Without a definition, G09 can be gamed by reclassifying backtest rows.Define: a forward bet is one whose game was unplayed at the moment the model line was written, recorded with a timestamp. Options in S-03.awaiting_owner_choice
DQ-07CorrelationAre same-game parlay legs correlated strongly enough to forbid independent pricing?Measured r = -0.181 (n=72) between side and total grades; correlation is negative.Whether the effect holds for player-prop legs, which were not captured.Parlay pricing maths depends on it; the D5 demotion trigger depends on it.Recompute after the forward sample; capture prop legs once a prop source exists.open
DQ-08DepthDoes depth deficiency actually move a line?Deficiency is captured for two clubs; n=0 games of outcome data.Effect size, and whether 2nd-string is materially worse than 4th-string.If it does not move a line, the whole depth register is decoration.Capture 30 more clubs and regress deficiency on result vs the model line over 100+ games.open
DQ-09LegalWhat is the legal ceiling on publishing these compiled CSVs?The NFL terms prohibit systematic retrieval to build a database absent consent; personal non-commercial use is permitted.Whether a public GitHub Pages site counts as non-commercial informational use in the NFL's view.If not, the public site must not publish the compiled tables.Owner decision; options in S-06.awaiting_owner_choice
DQ-10DepthWhich of the 32 clubs publish a usable depth chart at all?2 of 2 probed clubs publish one, both labelled unofficial.Whether the other 30 publish, and in what format.A league-wide deficiency signal needs uniform coverage.Probe all 32 club domains once; record which return a chart and which 404.open
DQ-11StakingHow should a push be treated in ROI?Pushes are excluded from the win-rate denominator and the ROI denominator.Whether the owner prefers push-as-half-loss, push-as-refund, or exclusion.Different conventions change ROI by a few percent, which is the size of the effect being chased.Owner decision; the convention is stated on every ROI table. Options in S-05.awaiting_owner_choice
DQ-12ModelIs a 13.5-point sigma for NFL margins still right for 2026?It is a declared constant, not fitted.The realised 2026 margin distribution.The moneyline probabilities inherit it directly.Fit sigma on weeks 1-9 and compare; publish both.open
