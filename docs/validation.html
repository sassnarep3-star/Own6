Validation & leaderboard

A candidate is not eligible for rewards until it clears every predeclared gate. Thresholds below are proposed governance choices, not sourced league facts or universal statistical standards.

Current leaderboard: no qualifying strategies. Empirical scores are blank because no dataset, candidate, test results, or audit trail exists.

Leaderboard rules

  • Freeze strategy, markets, stake sizing, odds availability, date range, inclusion rules, and metric before the scored period.
  • Rank eligible entries on risk-aware, net-of-price performance with uncertainty; show sample size, market count, return, drawdown, calibration and closing-line comparison where valid.
  • Keep a separate provisional area for candidates; no prize ranking from retrospective tuning. Use a held-out prospective period to determine rewards.
  • One account/strategy identity per ruleset, version score history, and disclose ties, voids, and exposure. Rewards policy itself requires legal and operational review.
  • Never treat an observed positive return as proof of skill alone. Continue monitoring post-selection and publish failures too.
