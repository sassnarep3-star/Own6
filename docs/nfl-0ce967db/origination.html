What the order means

  • Side first — home margin in points from the shrunk scoring-differential model plus 2.5 points of home field.
  • Total second — league mean total plus both clubs' shrunk offensive deviations minus both defensive deviations; published in two estimators until a walk-forward comparison separates them.
  • Moneyline third — the side probability through a normal CDF with σ fitted on prior games and bounded between 7 and 25 points by the registration.

All three are arithmetic on official season-to-date scoring. The inputs and their sample sizes are in data/nfl_0ce967db/week11_forecasts.csv and in the model-output ledger, one row per game per candidate.

Every number on this site is either read from the official nfl.com page linked beside it, or is arithmetic on such numbers with the derivation shown. Market prices are not published by any source in this evidence set, so every price, ROI and CLV cell is empty. No profitability claim is made anywhere. Sources & method.
