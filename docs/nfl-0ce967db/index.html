Read this before any number below. Every scored model shows a positive point estimate of Brier skill against a climatology control, and every one of them has a negative lower bound once whole weeks are resampled. So the apparent skill is not distinguishable from luck at this sample size. The removal-of-luck panel goes further and shows the models are also miscalibrated: they expect a 55% home-win rate in a season where home clubs won 46% of the games scored, because the registration adds a fixed +2.5 points of home field and 2026 has produced −0.53. That defect is measured, published and registered for a prospective fix — not quietly tuned away.
