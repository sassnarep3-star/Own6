If only three are done

  • SUG-06 — capture inactive lists on game day. It is the single largest evidence gap: it converts the injury report from a list of designations into a measurable play rate, and it is the prerequisite for the ablation the owner asked for by name.
  • SUG-07 — read one official game book and publish actual starters. One read settles whether starter data is obtainable at all before committing to 272 of them.
  • SUG-15 — raise the qualification sample from 100 to a full season. At the measured effect size, 100 games is very unlikely to separate skill from luck; the interval published on the board is the evidence for that.
