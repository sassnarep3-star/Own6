Read this first

  • Every number on this site is either copied from an official NFL page or arithmetic on numbers copied from them. The last column of every table is the page a row came from.
  • A blank cell is a finished answer: no source we are allowed to use supplies that value. Flagged for Review lists all 15 of them, including the places where the official page itself looks wrong.
  • There is no bookmaker here. Model prices are the only prices, so anything resembling profit is measured against our own number and is a calibration result, not an edge.
  • The graded window is Weeks 7-10 of 2026: 44 games. Every statistic publishes its n, because n=44 is the honest size of what is known.

Research and statistics only. No wager is placed, no pick is offered, and no profitability claim is made: the prices in this pack are the model's own, so a result measured against them is a calibration measurement, not an edge. Statistical validation reduces uncertainty; it cannot remove luck, which is why the leaderboard separates signal from staking and why the demotion gate can override a ranking.
