Opening and closing prices

An append-only snapshot ledger, with open, close and movement derived from the series.

Requirement 1 in full. The store is a ledger, not a field: one row per market per publication, never edited in place, each row chained to the previous one by a SHA-256 over its canonical form. Opening and closing numbers are derived from position in the series, so no one has to trust a label.
