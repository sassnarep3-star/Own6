Research and statistics only. No wager is placed, no pick is offered, and no profitability claim is made: the prices in this pack are the model's own, so a result measured against them is a calibration measurement, not an edge. Statistical validation reduces uncertainty; it cannot remove luck, which is why the leaderboard separates signal from staking and why the demotion gate can override a ranking. Every value comes from an official NFL page read on 2026-11-14 or from arithmetic on those values, published under the NFL.com Terms and Conditions for individual non-commercial review. A blank cell is blank because no official or licensed source supplies it - see Flagged for Review.
