Depth of replacement

How far down the list a club has to reach, and where a source actually says so.

Requirement 6: the depth of the replacement, not the fact of one. The report will not say who is first string, so this pack publishes two different things in two different columns and never confuses them: a lower bound derived from designations, and an evidenced tier where an official headline actually names the backup taking the job.

213 club-position groups. The bound is hard: every player the report rules Out or Doubtful has to be covered by someone further down that group's list. The deficit column is a declared weighting (1.0 unavailable, 0.25 questionable, 0.5 an evidenced replacement) and its score_kind says so. The complete table is in depth_of_replacement.csv.
