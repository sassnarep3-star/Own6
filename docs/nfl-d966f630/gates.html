Validation and demotion gates

What has to be true to be the reference line, and what takes that away.

Requirement 8. Ten gates over ten strategies, every observed value, threshold and verdict published, and the state each strategy ends in - with the failing gate named on the row that fails it.

Three blocks: the signal gates decide eligibility, the risk gates can send a live strategy back on their own even when its lines are accurate, and the integrity gates are an override that demotes everything in the same run if the price chain, the reconciliation or the feed identities fail.
