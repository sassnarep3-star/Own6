  • The Arizona abbreviation mismatch, which broke a run and is now recorded as data rather than silently mapped everywhere (see Sources & rights).
Research and statistics only. No wager is placed, no pick is offered, and no profitability claim is made: the prices in this pack are the model's own, so a result measured against them is a calibration measurement, not an edge. Statistical validation reduces uncertainty; it cannot remove luck, which is why the leaderboard separates signal from staking and why the demotion gate can override a ranking.
