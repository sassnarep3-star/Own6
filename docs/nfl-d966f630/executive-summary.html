What was asked for

Eight specific requirements were added to the standing brief: a price store that can say what a line opened and closed at; a verified results feed so that backtests and ROI are computable at all; a mandated origination order; fair, market and difference lines as separate columns; a leaderboard that will not let a staking run disguise a bad signal; depth of replacement rather than a yes/no injury flag; correlations only from official numbers, each with its n; and a gate that can send a live strategy back to paper.

What was built

  • Results feed - 44 official games (Weeks 7-10 2026) with both clubs, both point totals and the per-game URL; 37 identity checks (sum of wins against sum of losses, points for against points against, tie parity, unique ids, feed points never exceeding the season total) and 96 club-week reconciliations against the record badges printed on the game cards. All pass; 10 checks have no badge because the club was on that week's bye.
  • Price store - 210 append-only snapshot rows, hash-chained (the chain re-verifies with 0 breaks), with opening, intermediate, close-final and close-provisional roles derived from position in the series. 171 markets, of which 26 have a measurable movement, 118 have one snapshot and 14 have no number at all because the model could not originate a Week 7 moneyline.
  • Origination - side, then total, then moneyline, in that order and no other: asking for the total before the side exists raises. 14 Week 10 games priced, four model columns each (spread, total, moneyline, tie probability).
  • Three-column publication - model fair line, market line, difference, as separate columns on every row. The first is filled on every market the model can price; the other two are blank with a status string and a flag, because no source in this environment publishes a price.
  • Depth - 347 injury rows, 213 club-position groups, tiers-dropped lower bounds per unit, and 11 headline-evidenced replacement rows that name the tier a backup sits at where a headline actually says so.
  • Correlations - 8 measured rows, each with n (28 to 287), a Fisher interval, the method, the sample unit, one source URL per axis, and 5 excluded candidates with the reason and what would unlock them.
  • Backtest and leaderboard - 150 graded tickets and 166 settled legs across four bet families, then two rankings that are never averaged, and a reward tier decided by the signal block alone.
  • Demotion gate - 10 gates evaluated over all 10 strategies (100 rows), one promotion to live, nine held in paper, each with the failing gate, its observed value and its threshold named.

What the numbers say, in one paragraph each

The side is real; the total is not, yet. Over 44 graded games the published spread is 10.50 points of mean absolute error against a zero-margin line's 12.16 - a 1.66-point improvement, and the damping scan says no shrinkage helps (its best factor is 1.00). On the total the same scan runs the other way: 11.24 for our line against 10.63 for simply doubling the league's points-per-team, with the best shrink factor at 0.00. The scoring-rate decomposition adds noise to the total at this sample size, so the total-anchor strategy is the one the gate refuses to promote.

Separating signal from staking changed a decision. The moneyline-anchor strategy has the best skill score against its event's own base rate (Brier skill +0.0176, hit rate 73.3% on 30 graded legs) and is ranked first on signal - and it fails the calibration gate (gap 0.1222 against a 0.08 tolerance), so it stays in paper. The same-game side-plus-over parlay strategy is second on signal, and what holds it back is the risk block: an 11.00-unit drawdown against an 8.00-unit budget, on 13 tickets. The one strategy promoted to live, the plain side anchor, is ranked third on signal and has no failing gate at all. Had the board been ordered by units, the ranking would have been led by a moneyline run at n=30 and the ordering would have been about variance.

Luck is bounded, not removed. The side anchor's -3.00 units over 44 tickets is a z of -0.45 against what a fair price implies; the SGP's -9.00 over 13 tickets is z = -2.25 (p = 0.024). Neither number is treated as evidence about the model, and the staking columns cannot move a reward tier in either direction - the checker enforces that by refusing a payout column in the signal table and an accuracy column in the staking table.

What is missing and who has to decide

Ten suggestions and seven open questions are listed on Suggestions & questions, every one in state awaiting_human_choice with applied_by_default = no. The one that gates the most: a licensed market-price source. It is the only thing that turns the difference column, the edge statistics and closing-line value from blanks into measurements; four source classes have already been attempted and logged. The one that is self-serveable: backfilling Weeks 1-6 into the feed, which lifts n from 44 games to about 136 and dissolves the two sample-size flags without any new kind of data.
