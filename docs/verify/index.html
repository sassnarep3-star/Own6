How the audit was run

  1. Each published value was compared, one row at a time, with the official page for that row.
  2. The URL actually opened and the read date are stored on the row (ledger CSV in the repository).
  3. If the value could not be read from an official source, the row is not shown as verified: its published value stays blank on the Unverified page with the reason.
  4. The build fails if any ledger row has no official https link, an unknown status, or if an unverified row carries a value.
  5. Conflicts and rights problems are listed on Flagged for review; they do not block publication but are never hidden inside a table.

What this does and does not prove

It does not prove an edge. Verifying that a score, a standing or a statute is correct says nothing about whether a model can beat a market. On the single graded slate the market was at least as accurate as the model and every uncertainty interval included zero; no strategy passed its gates. Statistical validation reduces uncertainty about bad luck — it cannot remove luck from a small sample. See forward test and validation gates.

Verification limits

  • The 15 Nov 2 scores were verified row by row in this session. Of the 92 earlier scores, 13 were re-read by an earlier session and the rest carry a prior-session badge.
  • Market prices and prop lines are secondary-source (ESPN relay of DraftKings) and stay unverified: no official sportsbook or licensed odds page was read.
  • Aggregate cross-check: win-loss records recomputed from all 107 result rows match the official 30-team standings exactly.
