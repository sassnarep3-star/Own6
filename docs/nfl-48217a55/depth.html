REPLACEMENT DEPTH

What the official pages will and will not say

The brief asks for the depth of the replacement, not just the fact of one. The official injury report publishes the fact and not the depth: it names no replacement and gives no order. So this pack publishes floors - how many Out rows sit in each club and unit - and leaves the replacement column empty rather than filling it in.

This is the answer to resolve the depth-order source before building the feature. Route (a), the per-club depth page on nfl.com, does not exist: it returns 404. The roster route is live but publishes no depth order; it does link a depth chart on the club's own domain, which is a candidate route that is unverified for the other 31 clubs. Route (b), snap counts, found no official source. The feature is therefore not built.
