Why lower-bound scoring?

A strategy at +30% ROI over 15 bets has a lower bound below zero — it cannot outrank +4% over 500 bets. Ranking by the worst plausible skill estimate is the project's core anti-luck device, together with Bonferroni correction (G5) across every strategy ever tried, including abandoned ones.
