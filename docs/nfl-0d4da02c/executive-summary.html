The owner's brief contains 75 product requirements and 8 process instructions. Of those, 17 are cleared by evidence read this session from an official page, 5 are inherited from earlier packs as claims, 18 exist as schema or code with no data behind them, 32 are blocked by a named gate, 4 are partly cleared, 3 are open with no evidence either way, and 2 were rejected by the owner and are recorded so they are not re-proposed.

The three things that decide everything else

  1. No official market-price source exists. Until one does, the residual, closing line value and the vig decomposition cannot be published, and neither can any backtest that uses prices. The owner's own ordering rule (R-20) says decide the price policy first.
  2. No depth-order source is verified for all 32 clubs. Fourteen requirements sit behind this, including the entire backup-deficiency feature the owner asked for. The owner ordered this resolved before the feature is built, and that order is respected.
  3. No official inactive list has been captured. The injury register is now verifiable every week, but the question "did the player actually play" is unanswerable without it, so "Questionable" cannot be calibrated and no designation may become a model feature.

What is deliberately missing

No prices, no odds, no correlation between a designation and playing, no leaderboard rows, and no statement that any strategy is profitable. Those cells are blank because the evidence does not exist yet, and filling them would be the exact failure mode this project is built to avoid.

Research only. No prices, no profitability claim, and no wagers. Statistical validation can reduce uncertainty; it can never remove luck.
