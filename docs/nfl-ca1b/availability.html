What does 'Questionable' actually mean?

The official report gives no percentages, so they are measured here. Each designation row on the week-10 hub is joined by exact name to the official inactive list for the same game. Play rate = the share of those players who were not on the inactive list. Clubs whose game had no inactive article (Thursday and Monday games) are excluded from the denominator, never counted as healthy.
