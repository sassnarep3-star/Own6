The honest headline

Signal, honestly stated. The rating model picks the winner in 66 of 101 graded games (65.3%). Against a coin that is p = 0.0027. But under the model's own published probabilities the expected number of hits was 63.6, and the two-sided Poisson-binomial p is 0.674: the results are exactly what the published probabilities imply. That is the correct reading - a modest edge over a coin, and no evidence of a hidden extra edge.

Ranking. The game-bootstrap 95% interval on Brier skill has a point estimate of 0.1142 and a lower bound of -0.0069. The lower bound includes zero, so no rank is published and no strategy is reward-eligible.

Calibration. The raw model is overconfident: in its 80-100% band it forecast 0.895 and observed 0.636 over 22 games. A walk-forward variance multiplier (median k = 1.8, chosen only on data older than each prediction) is applied to every probability published in this pack, and the value of k is printed on each row.

What is deliberately blank

Every market price, every residual, every ROI and CLV, every leaderboard rank, every depth rank, and every player-level replacement claim. No official NFL page publishes any of them. Blanks are never filled by inference; each one carries the number that would clear it on Decision readiness and a row on Flagged for review.
