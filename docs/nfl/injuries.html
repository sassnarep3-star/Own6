Injury Tracking — timestamped official report

The system is designed to ingest the timestamped official injury report (Practice Report / Game Status / Inactive at 90 min) per policy PDF. DNP/Limited/Full and Out/Doubtful/Questionable are defined there. Every empirical cell is blank until licensed snapshots exist. Design: research/NFL_INJURY_DEPTH.md.
