Depth & Deficiencies — 2nd/3rd/4th-string backups replacing starters

Offense and defense deficiency tracking. Each unit (QB, OL, WR, RB, TE, DL, LB, DB, K) defines 1st/2nd/3rd/4th-string, a deficiency metric 1 − starter_snaps/expected, and a flag when a backup actually replaces a starter. Values are blank until a licensed depth-chart feed exists (F-NFL-04). Model: research/NFL_INJURY_DEPTH.md.

Deficiency → signal examples

  • QB deficiency >0.5 (QB2 starts) → downgrade passing efficiency (N04); flagged for CLV (N10).
  • OL deficiency >0.3 (two starters Out) → higher pressure, lower rushing efficiency (N01/N05; correlation C06).
  • Secondary missing CB1+CB2 → acute deficiency → elevate opponent WR projection; correlation C07.
