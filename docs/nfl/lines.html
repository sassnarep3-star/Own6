How a line is originated

  1. Possessions × offensive/defensive efficiency (EPA/points per drive) → expected points per team. Pace and efficiency definitions per Next Gen Stats.
  2. Home advantage & margin SD fit from licensed game log → win-probability distribution.
  3. Fair American price with no invented margin; de-vig market comparison on same line (DraftKings odds formulas).
  4. Player props: projected snaps × shrunk per-play rate; minutes only from official injury report (policy PDF).
  5. Combos/parlays: joint probability from training-only paired residuals via copula; independence is a labelled special case, never default (SciPy pearsonr).
