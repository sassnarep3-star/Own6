What “remove luck” means

Luck cannot be eliminated. The gates make it harder for a lucky streak to look like skill: pre-register thresholds, compare to the same-selection market, bootstrap whole-slate clusters, and adjust for every hypothesis tried (Bonferroni). See NIST CI and Bonferroni.
