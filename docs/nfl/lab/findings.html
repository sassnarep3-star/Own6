The three findings that matter most

The signal does not beat the close

A transparent efficiency model lost 1.2% per spread bet on 172 holdout bets, with an interval from -12.3% to +10.4%. That is not a near miss to be tuned away; it is what an information-free signal looks like after vig. Finding L-01.

Directional accuracy is not edge

The model gets the margin sign right 59.9% of the time and still loses money. The market gets it right 85.3% of the time because it already knows what the model knows. Finding L-10.

Most of the combo space is data-blocked

Five of eight candidates cannot be evaluated because the archives do not exist: opening lines, prop outcomes, prop prices and dated injuries. Finding L-11.

Dependence is measurable, and small

Spread-cover vs over: phi +0.022 over 2,792 games, ratio 1.023. Cross-game pairs show +0.099 at n=234 with an interval through zero - a useful picture of how noisy parlay samples are. Findings L-04 and L-05.
