How the own line is built

Ratings

  • Points for / against, opponent-adjusted by alternating least squares, fitted on every season before the test season only.
  • Season decay 0.55 per year, so last year counts about half of this year.
  • Shrinkage 6 games toward league average, so a two-game team is not treated as a full season.
  • Home field is estimated from the data (about 2.0 points in the window), not hard-coded.

Dispersion

  • Margin SD is measured out-of-sample on the holdout season (13.9 points), not from the fit.
  • Total SD is set 6% wider than margin SD as a stated assumption.
  • Pushes on whole-number lines are reported separately; they are never split 50/50.

What would make these lines useful

A verified closing price. With it, every row gets a fair-price comparison, a de-vigged edge, and a closing-line-value check - which is the only way the line becomes a decision rather than a description. See SG-03 and Q03.
