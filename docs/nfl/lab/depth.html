How the index works (and what it is not)

The formula

deficiency = position weight x string penalty x status risk

  • Position weight: QB 1.00, LT 0.45, RT 0.35, C 0.30, WR 0.35, CB/DE 0.40, S 0.32, LB 0.30, RB 0.30, K 0.20, KR/PR 0.05. A modelling choice, published in full and editable in one dict.
  • String penalty: 2nd string 0.35, 3rd 0.62, 4th 0.80 - the cost of replacing a starter with that depth.
  • Status risk: Out 1.0, Doubtful 0.75, Questionable 0.45, plus half the practice risk (DNP 0.65, limited 0.30).

What it is not

  • Not a measured points value. There is no historical injury archive to regress against (Q10).
  • Not a snap-share model: no snap counts were obtained.
  • Not league-wide: 29 clubs are missing (F-N06).
  • Not stable: a Wednesday practice status is not a Sunday status.

What the index is actually made of

Only rows with a non-zero index move a club's number. Everything else on the board is a full-participation starter or a 1-string replacement, and a 1-string replacement carries no penalty in the published formula - which is a stated limitation, not a finding: losing a starter to a same-position backup may still matter, and the index cannot see it.
