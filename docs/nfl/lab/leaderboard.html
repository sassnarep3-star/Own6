Why no money: a monetary reward creates pressure to loosen the gates exactly when the evidence is weakest. Research priority is a real reward inside a research programme and cannot be gamed by variance.

The rules that keep it honest

  • Frozen family. All candidates are declared before the run; adding one after seeing results is a new, separately-adjusted test.
  • Lower bound ranking. Rank by the 5th percentile of the clustered bootstrap, never the point estimate.
  • Multiplicity. Bonferroni across every candidate tested, including the failures.
  • Deflated Sharpe. The best of eight trials must clear the expected maximum under eight trials.
  • No promotion on one season. A single holdout can reject a strategy; it cannot prove one.

See Validation for the gate definitions and Q14 for why the ranking rule is what it is.
