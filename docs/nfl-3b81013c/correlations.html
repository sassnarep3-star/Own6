What the bounds prove

  • Two 0.50 legs have a joint probability anywhere from 0.00 to 0.50, so the independence product of 0.25 sits in the middle of a very wide range. A price built on independence can be wrong in either direction.
  • The spread between the floor and ceiling is the honest uncertainty about a parlay until a correlation is measured.
  • Some pairings are definitionally redundant rather than correlated, such as a cornerback interception prop and the team interception total; those must be excluded by rule, not corrected by a coefficient.
  • A Fisher-z interval and a slate-clustered bootstrap are both implemented, because a correlation computed on within-week data is not independent across rows.
