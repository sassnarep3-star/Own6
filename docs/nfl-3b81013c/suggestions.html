Order of operations this session recommends

  1. Decide the data-rights question first, because it gates everything else and cannot be worked around.
  2. If rights are settled, buy the odds feed before the play-by-play feed: without a price there is nothing to be right or wrong about.
  3. Only then calibrate the depth weights, because a weight without a price has no decision value.
  4. Keep the empty leaderboard until a strategy clears all ten gates; an early ranking is worse than no ranking.
