Leaderboard competition

Reward tiers and the deliberately empty ranking

A competition frame that pays only after a strategy proves itself against a clustered interval, a multiplicity correction, an out-of-sample proper scoring rule and a de-vigged closing line.

The leaderboard is empty by design. A rank is awarded only when all ten gates pass. With zero authorized observations and no licensed market feed, no strategy can pass, so every row shows a blank rank and the quarantine tier.

What would have to be true for a first rank to appear

  1. A licensed or explicitly permitted data path exists, satisfying the terms of use (gate VG-01).
  2. At least 200 graded events are recorded, spanning at least 18 weekly slates (gate VG-04).
  3. Every observation carries a timestamp before kickoff and consistent with the T-90 minute report (gate VG-02).
  4. A frozen walk-forward run beats the de-vigged market on a proper scoring rule (gates VG-03 and VG-05).
  5. De-vigged same-line closing value is positive (gate VG-06).
  6. For a parlay or prop combo, a training-only paired-residual correlation exists (gate VG-07).
  7. The Holm-adjusted p-value clears 0.05 and the slate-clustered ROI lower bound clears zero (gate VG-08).
  8. The incoming depth strings behind any depth signal are officially confirmed (gate VG-09).
