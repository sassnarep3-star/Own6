What the league's designations actually mean

The league's injury report prints three game-status designations — Out, Doubtful and Questionable — and never prints a percentage. This page measures each one against the official Game Book of the same week: of the players the league designated, how many actually started, how many came off the bench, how many were inactive and how many did not play.

The play rate is the share of designated players the official Game Book shows actually played, and the interval is a Wilson 95% interval on that share. n is the number of designated players who could be joined to an official Game Book of the same week; it is printed on every row so a rate is never read without its sample size.
