The demotion gate is the rule that sends a live strategy back to paper. It is armed with six rules, each with a numeric threshold. Right now every rule reports armed_not_evaluable because there are no live strategies — which is itself the correct state: the gate blocks promotion before it has to block demotion.

At -110 the break-even win rate is 52.38%. To show a real 5-point edge at 99% confidence and 80% power takes about 1,160 settled bets — roughly six seasons at 200 bets a year. This is why the leaderboard is ranked on probability scoring rather than on win rate, and why closing-line value (suggestion A10-S06) is the better early metric: it converges without waiting for results.
