Depth of replacement is the requirement, not just "who is out". A 4th-string guard starting is a different number from a 2nd-string guard starting. This pack resolves that by joining the official injury report to the official club depth chart and recording which string the club has to reach. Only 4 of 32 club sites rendered their chart as text, so only 18 of 77 Out-or-Doubtful players have a resolved replacement. The rest are left blank and flagged — not guessed.
