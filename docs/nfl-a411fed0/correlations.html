How to read these

  • A4-C01 / A4-C02 are fit diagnostics, not skill. They are in-sample by construction and will look better than the model really is.
  • A4-C03 is the parlay-pricing number. If it is genuinely negative, a favourite-plus-over parlay is less likely than the independent product implies, and the naive parlay price is too generous to us.
  • A4-C09 is the luck test: how stable a team's scoring margin is between the first three weeks and weeks 7-9. At 0.30 it says most of early-season margin is noise, which is why the ratings need shrinking (suggestion A10-S04).
  • A4-C10 has n = 23 and is marked insufficient_n. It is the one we most want to be true and the one we can least afford to act on.
