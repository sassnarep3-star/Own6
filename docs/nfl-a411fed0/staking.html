Signal vs Staking

Keeping a lucky staking run from masking a bad signal.

Why these columns are separate. A staking plan can manufacture a good-looking P&L out of a losing signal, right up until a losing streak ends it. The clearest demonstration is in this pack: strategy A9-S05 (home underdog) hit only 34% of its picks — a badly losing signal — and under flat staking it lost 15.4 units. Under a doubling-up plan it showed a paper profit of +48 units before a drawdown of 511 units. The signal is identical in both columns. Only the staking changed.
