Read this first. This pack originates its own NFL lines, but it has no market prices. No page on nfl.com or on any NFL club site publishes point spreads, totals or moneylines, so the price-snapshot store has 84 originated markets and zero prices. That makes return-on-investment, closing-line value and settlement all uncomputable, and it fails gate A4-G05, which is the gate that must pass before anything leaves paper. Everything here is therefore paper only, ranked on probability scoring rather than on profit. See Price Snapshots and flag A4-F02.

What this pack does

Origination runs in a fixed order — side first, then total, then moneyline — so that step 3 is derived from steps 1 and 2 rather than fitted independently. The side comes from a least-squares margin rating fitted on verified finals; the total from a separate rating fitted on verified game totals; the moneyline from the margin plus the model's own residual distribution. Every market is published as three separate columns: the model's fair line, the market line, and the difference between them.
