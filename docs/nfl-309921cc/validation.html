Validation gates

Twelve gates. Four are open because they depend only on how the pack is built. Eight are blocked because a licensed input does not exist in this repository. A blocked gate is not a failure — it is the reason no performance number is published.

gate idgaterulehow it removes luckblocking missing inputstatusofficial or method reference
G01Rights and provenanceEvery input row must trace to an official NFL or club page read in this session, and the pack must be non-commercial and informational only.Does not remove luck; it prevents a result that cannot be lawfully reproduced from ever reaching the board.opensource ↗
G02No invented market pricesA strategy may not be graded against any price this repository did not obtain under licence. Reference prices stay blank.Prevents a fabricated benchmark from manufacturing apparent skill.Licensed historical price archive.blockedsource ↗
G03Minimum sampleAt least 200 graded selections per strategy family before any performance statistic is published.Small samples are dominated by variance; the floor stops a short lucky run from qualifying.Graded selection history.blockedsource ↗
G04Chronological out-of-sampleParameters are fitted on seasons strictly before the test season; no random k-fold and no same-season fitting.Removes in-sample optimism, the most common way luck is mistaken for skill.Multi-season results and price history.blockedsource ↗
G05Pre-registrationHypothesis, signal rule, stop rule and parameter values are recorded before any selection is graded.Removes post-hoc rule-shopping, which turns noise into apparent signal.opensource ↗
G06Multiple-testing controlHolm-Bonferroni correction across the whole strategy family at family-wise alpha = 0.05.Ten strategies tested at 5% produce a false positive far more often than 5% of the time.Test statistics from G03/G04.blockedsource ↗
G07Calibration and proper scoringForecasts are scored with Brier score and log loss against a stated baseline, and reliability is reported.A strategy can win bets and still be badly calibrated; proper scores expose that.Graded probability-outcome pairs.blockedsource ↗
G08Closing-line valueReport the difference between the price taken and the closing price.Beating the close is the standard test that separates information from variance.Licensed closing prices.blockedsource ↗
G09Dependence handled, never assumedEvery parlay and same-game combo price is built from a measured dependence. Independence is never an input.Assuming independence in a correlated combo misprices the joint event in one direction only.Paired licensed residual history for each leg pair.blockedsource ↗
G10Luck-separated skill statisticThe lower bound of a slate-clustered 95% confidence interval on the skill statistic must exceed zero.A point estimate above zero is not evidence; the interval must exclude zero after clustering by slate.Graded selections with slate identifiers.blockedsource ↗
G11Market-integrity eligibilityA market family tied to officiating or injuries, or knowable in advance or manipulable by one person, is permanently ineligible.Keeps the catalog inside the league's own integrity policy rather than testing the boundary.opensource ↗
G12ReproducibilityOne deterministic command rebuilds every table; seeds are fixed; all inputs are versioned in git.A result that cannot be recomputed cannot be audited, so it cannot count as evidence.opensource ↗
