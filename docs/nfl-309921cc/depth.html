Depth charts and starter-versus-backup deficiency

Both clubs label their own chart unofficial. Patriots.com says the chart "is unofficial and is published by the writers of Patriots.com"; the Lions chart is an "Unofficial Depth Chart Compiled by Lions Football Communications". That caveat is carried onto every row below, and the deficiency score is a proposed weighting, not an observed quantity (flag NFL3-F07). Offense and defense are tracked the same way.

Deficiency map

For each position slot the shallowest listed string not ruled Out is the active string; the next listed player behind them is the replacement. A slot scores 0.00 when a healthy 1st string is active, 0.35 when the 1st string is Questionable, 0.65 when a healthy 2nd string must start, 0.85 when the 2nd string is himself Questionable, and 1.00 when the replacement is 3rd string or deeper, or when no replacement is listed at all. Those weights are design choices.
