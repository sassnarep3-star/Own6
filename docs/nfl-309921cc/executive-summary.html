Executive summary

The short version

This pack can be summarised in one sentence: the framework, the measured league-level statistics and the verified Week 10 picture are real; every betting-performance number is blank and stays blank until licensed data exists.

What is verified

  • All 32 club standings rows, read from nfl.com/standings on 2026-11-13, with the twelve columns that the page's own legend defines.
  • The 14-game Week 10 slate with kickoff times, networks, pre-game records and canonical game URLs, from nfl.com/schedules, plus 4 bye teams.
  • All 326 rows of the official Week 10 injury report from nfl.com/injuries, with the five columns the page defines.
  • Two club depth charts (Patriots, Lions) giving 1st-to-4th string ordering for offense and defense — with both clubs' own "unofficial" disclaimer carried onto every row.
  • Eight correlations and a 32-row luck index, computed here from the verified standings table.

What is deliberately blank

  • Every market price, closing line and closing-line value (no licensed odds archive).
  • Every moneyline probability (needs a victory-margin distribution that no official page supplies).
  • Every measured dependence between two betting legs (needs graded paired outcomes).
  • Every strategy performance statistic: the ledger holds 0 graded selections.

The four findings that change what you should do next

  1. Rights is the first question, not the last. The NFL terms prohibit systematic retrieval to compile a database; this repository is, by construction, a compiled database of official tables. Nothing else should grow until that is answered.
  2. 71 citations point at a dead URL. nfl.com/official-nfl-rules returned the official NFL 404 page on 2026-11-13, and 20+ files in this repository — including live published pages — cite it as a source.
  3. The standings lag a completed game. Thursday's Commanders 24–21 win over the Giants is published as final, but the standings table still shows both clubs with pre-game records. Any model mixing the two is internally inconsistent.
  4. Four NFL packs now compete. A reviewer can easily act on the older one. Nothing is deleted here; the fix is a canonical pointer.

Originated lines

Thirteen scheduled Week 10 games carry our own fair spread and total, computed only from verified points-for and points-against per game. The model's mean total is 46.3785 against the league's own 45.9197 points per game computed from the standings — a difference of 0.4588 points, which is the model's self-consistency check. The one game already final is not modelled.

Luck removal, demonstrated

Regressing win percentage on net points per game across the 32 official rows gives R² = 0.7710 (slope 0.033574 per net point per game, p = 4.07501e-11). The residual is the part of a team's record that its scoring does not explain — the same estimator is what gate G10 applies to a strategy, but on slate-clustered data, and it requires the interval to exclude zero before any reward.
