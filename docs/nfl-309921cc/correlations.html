Unlike every other NFL pack in this repository, these correlation values are measured: each is computed here from the 32 verified official standings rows, with n, a two-sided p-value, a Fisher z interval and a 5,000-draw percentile bootstrap interval computed independently. They describe the league, not any betting market, and they are not betting edges.

Two of the eight are not statistically distinguishable from zero at the 5% level (points-for vs points-against, and division record vs overall record). That result is published as it came out; it is not suppressed.
