Release gate

The system is designed to fail closed.

Calculations and synthetic tests demonstrate software behavior only. They do not establish a real NFL edge, profitable strategy, or a correlation coefficient.

  • No licensed timestamped odds archive or NFL play-by-play feed is configured.
  • Empirical line, ROI, CLV, and combo-correlation results are not available.
  • Injury and depth tables are a dated snapshot, not a live lineup service.
  • Rewards remain disabled until a strategy clears every required gate.

Research & software verification only. This dashboard does not provide picks, betting advice, or a guarantee that skill can remove luck.
