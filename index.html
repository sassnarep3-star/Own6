Research strategy archetypes · proposed

Executive summary

Price outcomes as probability distributions, account for uncertainty and compare against executable market prices net of vig. Single bets need calibrated marginal probabilities; parlays and player-prop combos need calibrated joint probabilities. Correlation is context-dependent: estimate it from paired, out-of-sample residuals rather than assuming independence or inserting a guessed number.

Leaderboard rewards should prioritize durable predictive skill—not raw wins. Score strictly out of sample, record each forecast before tipoff, benchmark against closing market prices, and publish uncertainty, sample size, calibration and drawdown alongside returns. Penalize selection bias, stale prices and repeated testing.

How skill is isolated

  • Pre-register features, selection rules and evaluation period.
  • Use rolling time splits; never train on future information.
  • Compare to market and simple baselines after vig.
  • Report calibration, log loss/Brier score, ROI and uncertainty.
  • Require robustness across seasons and sensitivity checks.

Responsible scope

No promise of profit is possible. Past outcomes do not guarantee future results. Follow local laws, sportsbook terms and age restrictions; set limits and do not chase losses. A strategy that fails a gate should be withheld from leaderboard rewards.
