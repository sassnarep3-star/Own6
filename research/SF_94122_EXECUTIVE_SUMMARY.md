## 1. Bottom-Line Executive Recommendations

1. **Current Governing Rate (`1.6%` through February 28, 2027):**  
   For multi-unit residential buildings in San Francisco (`94122`) with a Certificate of Occupancy on or before **June 13, 1979**, **San Francisco Administrative Code Chapter 37** controls and supersedes California AB 1482's higher `8.8%` statewide cap ([SF.gov Rent Increases](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco); [SF.gov AB 1482](https://www.sf.gov/reports--california-tenant-protection-act-2019-ab-1482); [Cal. Civ. Code § 1947.12(d)(3)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=1947.12.&lawCode=CIV)). For effective dates **March 1, 2026 through February 28, 2027**, the San Francisco Rent Board's official Allowable Annual Rent Increase is **1.6%** (`60%` of Bay Area CPI-U), applied strictly to lawful base rent **without rounding up** ([Form 571 PDF](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/571_Allowable_Annual_Increases_26-27_1.pdf)).
2. **Mandatory Pre-Notice Gate — Rent Increase License (`portal.sfrb.org`):**  
   Since **March 1, 2023**, owners of 1–9 unit buildings must report unit occupancy/rent information annually by **March 1** in the [SF Rent Board Housing Inventory](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory) to receive an active **Rent Increase License** (`SF Admin Code § 37.15`). Serving or making effective an annual or banked rent increase without an active license renders the increase **null and void** ([SF.gov Noncompliance Notice](https://www.sf.gov/notice-of-owner-noncompliance-with-housing-inventory)). In **Appeal `AL260003` (`2395 – 47th Avenue`, Outer Sunset, Feb. 10, 2026)**, the Rent Board voted **5–0** to affirm **$14,742.04** in tenant overpayment refunds even though the landlord acted in good faith under property-tax and insurance burdens ([Feb. 10, 2026 Rent Board Minutes PDF](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/m021026.pdf)).
3. **Recommended Building-Wide Policy (Passes Strict SF Tenant-Lawyer Scrutiny):**  
   Adopt a **written, neutral, building-wide policy** applying the **standard current annual Rent Board allowance (`1.6%`)** to **both units** on each unit's first legally eligible periodic date after confirming active licensing, lease status, and zero open habitability/retaliation bars:
   - **Unit 2 (`$3,150.00/mo` reported since `June 1, 2019`, `Tenant 2A` & `Tenant 2B`):** Once the 2019–2026 rent ledger confirms `$3,150.00` is the current month-to-month base rent, a **1.6% annual adjustment (`+$50.40/month` → `$3,200.40/month`)** on 30-day personal / 35-day mailed written notice is unmistakably reasonable, avoids a >10% 90-day shock in high-scrutiny ZIP `94122`, and **preserves all 6 prior banked years (`11.5%`) intact** under SF Rent Board partial-increase banking rules ([SF.gov Banked Rent Increases](https://www.sf.gov/information--banked-rent-increases)).
   - **Unit 1 (`$3,995.00/mo`, `February 1, 2025` initial lease; `April 1, 2026` continuation with `Tenant 1A` & `Tenant 1D`):**
     - **Roommate Rule:** Because original occupant `Tenant 1A` remains in continuous possession, **Costa-Hawkins vacancy decontrol (`Cal. Civ. Code § 1954.53(d)(3)`) and Rent Board Rule `6.14` do NOT apply**, and **zero extra rent** may be charged for `Tenant 1D` ([SF.gov Section 6.14 & Costa-Hawkins](https://www.sf.gov/information--rent-increases-under-section-614-and-costa-hawkins)).
     - **Contract Gate (`unverified`):** Inspect the signed `April 1, 2026` document first. **If** it created a new 12-month fixed term (`April 1, 2026 – March 31, 2027`) with no rent-increase reservation clause, **no unilateral increase may take effect before `April 1, 2027`** ([Cal. Civ. Code § 827](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=827.&lawCode=CIV)), at which point the upcoming `2027–2028` Rent Board rate (`unverified` as of Nov. 6, 2026) will govern. **If** Unit 1 is currently month-to-month with zero increases since `February 1, 2025`, waiting until the **24-month anniversary on `February 1, 2027`** (serving 35-day mailed notice by `December 28, 2026` or 30-day personal notice by `January 2, 2027`) allows applying **1.6% (`+$63.92/month` → `$4,058.92/month`)** while banking `February 1, 2026`'s `1.4%` (whereas raising *before* `February 1, 2027` permanently forfeits that `1.4%` under [SF.gov Irregular Periods](https://www.sf.gov/information--increasing-rents-during-irregular-periods)).

## 3. Corporate REITs vs. Individual SF Property Owners

- **How Large Real Estate Corporations Operate in San Francisco:**  
  Public multifamily REITs such as **AvalonBay Communities** and **Equity Residential** (~184,000 combined apartment homes) reported **4.1% Q2 2026** and **4.8% July 2026** portfolio renewal effective rent increases (`6.5%–7.3%` in Northern California/SF market-rate communities) in their [July 22, 2026 SEC Form 425 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/915912/000110465926085858/tm2617036d4_425.htm)—figures driven by **post-1995 Costa-Hawkins-exempt communities**, NOT pre-1979 rent-controlled duplexes. On SF rent-controlled properties, institutional operators rely on ERP calendar automation to file bulk Housing Inventory templates before **March 1** and serve exact **annual AGA increases (`1.6%`) every 12 months**, preventing large banked backlogs while filing formal Rent Board petitions for capital improvements or bond passthroughs ([SF.gov Petitions](https://www.sf.gov/landlord-petitions-and-passthroughs)). At the same time, corporate operators face federal/state antitrust prosecution over algorithmic pricing ([U.S. & California v. RealPage](https://www.justice.gov/atr/media/1383471/dl)), San Francisco's citywide ban on algorithmic rent-setting devices ([SF Admin Code § 37.10C](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/san_francisco/latest/sf_admin/0-0-0-70856)), and California Attorney General enforcement against fee unbundling and electronic-only payment mandates ([CA AG Tenant Guidance](https://oag.ca.gov/tenants)).
- **How Individual SF Property Owners Operate (and Trip Procedural Landmines):**  
  As documented in the [June 10, 2025 SF Rent Board Minutes (`m061025.pdf`)](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/m061025.pdf), individual SF landlords frequently leave rents unchanged for many years (`Unit 2` at `$3,150` since `2019`) to keep reliable tenants, then attempt an informal or banked catch-up when taxes and insurance rise—often **without an active Housing Inventory license**, **via email/text**, **rounded to whole dollars**, or **via informal roommate agreements**, all of which are void and trigger multi-year overpayment refunds (`AL260003`, `$14,742.04` refund in Outer Sunset; `AL250027`, non-waivable Costa-Hawkins protection). *(Note: No government agency publishes an official citywide mean actual increase % by owner size on SF rent-controlled stock; that metric is left blank and flagged `unverified`.)*

## 4. Correct Way to Inform Tenants & Permissible Reasons

1. **Formal Written Paper Notice & Statutory Service Only (`Cal. Civ. Code § 827`):**  
   Per the [California Attorney General](https://oag.ca.gov/tenants) and [Cal. Civ. Code § 827(b)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=827.&lawCode=CIV), **phone calls, text messages, and emails are NOT valid legal notice**. Serve a signed written notice to all adult tenants on the lease either **(a) by personal delivery** (`30` calendar days for `<=10%`; `90` calendar days for `>10%`) or **(b) by first-class U.S. mail within California** (`+5` calendar days under `CCP § 1013` = **`35` days** for `<=10%` or **`95` days** for `>10%`), keeping a signed Proof of Service and USPS Certificate of Mailing.
2. **Mandatory Notice Elements:**  
   State the current lawful base rent, exact unrounded dollar increase, exact percentage (using the rate in effect on the **effective date**), new monthly base rent, effective date, and—if any banked percentage is included—an explicit written itemization of the banked years vs. current annual percentage ([SF.gov Banked Rent Increases](https://www.sf.gov/information--banked-rent-increases)).
3. **Reasonable, Tenant-Lawyer-Safe Reasons:**  
   Under `SF Admin Code § 37.3(a)(1)`, no narrative justification is required beyond citing the statutory annual CPI-indexed allowance (`1.6%` for `3/1/2026–2/28/2027`). Do **not** cite rising property taxes or insurance to exceed `1.6%` without an approved O&M Petition or G.O. Bond Worksheet (`AL260003`), and **never** cite roommate changes (`Tenant 1D`), repair requests (`Cal. Civ. Code § 1942.5` 180-day retaliation bar), or market rents.

## 5. Passing SF Tenant-Lawyer Scrutiny & Raising One Unit vs. Both Units

- **Why ZIP `94122` Receives Heightened Tenant-Advocate Scrutiny:**  
  In the SF Rent Board's [2025 Annual Buyout Report](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/2025_Rent_Board_Annual_Report_on_Buyout_Agreements_Amended.pdf), **`94122 – Sunset` ranked #2 citywide in Pre-Buyout Declarations (`48` of `502`) and tied #1 citywide in Buyout Agreements (`25` of `278`)**. Tenants in `94122` have free access to the Housing Rights Committee of SF, Bay Area Legal Aid, Bill Sorro Housing Program, Eviction Defense Collaborative ([SF.gov Tenant & Landlord Resources](https://www.sf.gov/tenant-and-landlord-resources)), and free, no-time-limit Rent Board Form 516C / Form 518 petitions.
- **One Unit vs. Both Units — Avoiding Discrimination & Retaliation Claims:**  
  Across the **Federal Fair Housing Act** ([42 U.S.C. § 3601 et seq.](https://www.justice.gov/crt/fair-housing-act-1)), **California FEHA** ([Cal. Gov. Code § 12955](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=12955.&lawCode=GOV)), and **SF Police Code § 3304** ([AmLegal § 3304](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/san_francisco/latest/sf_police/0-0-0-7119)), **19+ protected characteristics** (race, national origin, immigration status, primary language, age, disability, familial/marital status, sex/LGBTQ+, religion, source of income/vouchers, veteran status, height/weight) are protected against differential rental terms.
  - **Raising ONLY one unit when both are legally eligible** creates an immediate 1-to-1 comparator in a duplex if the two households differ in any protected trait (e.g., age, national origin, family composition, income source) or recent maintenance history.
  - **Gold-Standard Solution:** Establish a **single written, neutral, building-wide policy** applying the **standard `1.6%` current SF Rent Board annual allowance** to **both units** on each unit's objective eligible date (`+$50.40/mo` on Unit 2 once month-to-month ledger & license are verified; `+$63.92/mo` on Unit 1 on `February 1, 2027` if month-to-month, or upon expiration of its `April 1, 2026` fixed term if locked through `March 31, 2027`). If Unit 1 is contractually locked in a fixed term while Unit 2 is month-to-month, raising Unit 2 now (`1.6%`) while contemporaneously scheduling Unit 1 for the identical formula at lease expiration is 100% objective, lawful, and defensible.
